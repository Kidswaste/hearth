# Round 143 · lab143: the Lab regrouped, more right-click menus, and a Forgeheart look

The owner asked: "Would be nice if the whole lab was lighter and with less tiny options everywhere, although they are
appreciated. But with every changes that have been added I think it would be worth grouping some of them together,
making it more navigable, add more context options etc. Keep the look sleak and 'forgeheart themed' but feel free to be
creative with UI/UX and take inspiration from my moodboards in Hearth (scanlines / moving objects / color)".

What this round does:

1. **Folds.** Related small buttons sit behind one labelled button each: **⚙ Inspect ▾** in the Lab bar, **♩ Beat ▾** and
   **⚡ Sync ▾** on the timeline. A fold holds the *real* controls (moved, not copied: the same element, classes,
   `data-feature`, listeners and keys), shown in a small floating tray on click; Esc or a click outside folds it. The
   grid row is no longer a row of its own (it lives in ♩ Beat ▾), so the timeline gets that height back.
2. **More right-click.** A timeline track has its own menu now; the ruler, the preview and a layer row got the rows they
   lacked (inside their existing submenus where a menu was already at its size budget, dev/checks/menucalm.js). Every row calls something that already existed.
3. **The Forgeheart look.** Static scanlines on the panel chrome, a slow glint along the timeline and the Layers & knobs
   head, an ember playhead that breathes while stopped, ember / AI-violet accents. CSS only, still while the scene
   plays, off under reduced motion. **Lab ⋯ › View & help › Lab look: Forgeheart · Plain** switches it.

Nothing was removed: every action is still one or two clicks away, and the keys (T, K, S, H, Space, F…) are unchanged.

### Ways in for every moved control

- **Click the fold** (⚙ Inspect ▾ / ♩ Beat ▾ / ⚡ Sync ▾): the tray with the real controls.
- **Right-click the fold**: the same controls as a keyboard-navigable menu (↑ ↓ Enter, Esc), each row clicking the real one.
- **Right-click the timeline's empty space** (the Lab timeline menu, declutter-lab.js): **♩ Beat ›** and **⚡ Sync ›**
  list the same rows, beside Live, Triggers, presets, Auto bars that were already there.
- **Keys** are unchanged: T (tap), K / S / H, Space, Shift+L (live sound), \` (console).
- **Ctrl/⌘+;** (the command bar) and 🔎 Lab search still reach everything they did (they read the same menus and
  buttons).

## The context menus

One shared, keyboard-navigable menu was already there and is reused: `showMenu` (the preview, the layer row, the folds'
right-click) and `popMenu` (the timeline, the knobs) from renderer.js, both with ↑ ↓ Enter, ← back, a filter field on
long menus and Esc. The timeline's new track menu moved off the older `menuAt` (mouse-only) onto `popMenu`.

## The look

`tools/three-regroup.css`, under `html:not([data-lablook="plain"])`:

- **Scanlines**: a static `repeating-linear-gradient` (1 px every 3 px, 3.5 % white) as a `::before` *behind* each
  panel's content (`isolation: isolate; z-index: -1`) on the Lab bar, the timeline and the Layers & knobs panel. Never
  over the preview's picture (checked). A thin ember → violet rim on their top edge, and two faint corner glows.
- **Moving**: a 1 px glint drifting along the top of the timeline and the Layers & knobs head (`transform`, 9 s); the
  playhead is an ember gradient with a soft halo whose `opacity` breathes (2.8 s). The open fold and lit toolbar
  buttons get ember / violet accents; the selected layer row an ember edge.
- **Cost**: no JS timers, no per-frame JS; animations on `transform` / `opacity` only. While the scene plays
  (`html.lab-playing`, round 142's LabPlay signal) every loop is `animation-play-state: paused`. Off under
  `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` and Hearth's own Appearance › Motion **calm** / **off** (`data-motion`).
- **Lab ⋯ › View & help › Lab look: Forgeheart / Plain** (store `three.labLook`, `html[data-lablook]`): Plain removes
  the scanlines, rims, glints and ember.

## Weight

The two new files (`tools/three-regroup.js` ≈ 10 KB, `tools/three-regroup.css` ≈ 8 KB) load with the lazy group `lab`
(after the first screen, with the rest of the Lab). Nothing was added in front of the first screen;
`node dev/lazy-manifest.js --check` is clean.

## The check

`dev/checks/lab143.js` (on realscene-lib's scene): every moved control is the real one, hidden while folded, drawn when
its fold opens and listed in its fold's menu (and Console works from it); what stays is drawn; a fold opens with a
click and folds with Esc and a click outside; real right-clicks on a layer row, a track, the ruler, the preview, a knob
and the Quality chip open their menus with the new rows, Esc closes each; the look's loops run when stopped, pause while
playing, are off under reduced motion and Motion: off; Plain turns it off. Pictures: `lab143.png`, `lab143-beat.png`.
