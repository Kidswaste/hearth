# Tour (round 15): Hearth helps you discover what it can do

You said "feel free to make me discover things". Hearth now has about 10,000 upgrades you haven't seen. This round
shows them to you a few at a time, picked for how you work: what's new after an update, short guided tours with a
ring on the real buttons, at most one tip a day, a map of what you have and haven't tried, and ideas for tonight.

It all stays out of your way. When Hearth opens, the most you get is one small card, and only after an update. There
are no new buttons on screen. It all runs on your computer and costs no tokens: Hearth reads the upgrade notes from
its own folder and your habits from its usage data, sends nothing to an engine and adds nothing to any prompt.
Start with `/whats-new`, `/tour renders` or ☰ Commands › ✦ Discover.

## What's new
1. **After an ⬆ Update, one small card** (bottom left) shows the 5 new things that fit how you work best.
2. **Only the new lines.** Before updating, Hearth notes how far each upgrade note went. After the update, it shows only the lines that are new. A note that didn't exist before counts as entirely new.
3. **Picked by your habits.** The Lab, sliders, Shuffle / Save, Tap, beats and songs, Freeze, frame sizes and the Three Director rank first. The buttons and commands you use most this week add to that.
4. **Spread across areas**: at most two picks from one part of a note and three from one note, so a single area can't fill the card.
5. **▶ Show me** on every line: if the control is on screen, it gets a pulsing ring.
6. Otherwise, Show me runs the line's own command when it only opens or shows something (`/renders`, `/apps discover`, `/access log`…).
7. A command that would change or make something goes into the chat box instead, so you press Enter yourself.
8. For a keyboard shortcut, Show me rings the keys button and shows the keys.
9. When none of that applies, Show me starts the guided tour for that area (render queue, board eyes, apps, access, sequence, keys).
10. **× Not for me** removes the line. Lines of that kind sink from then on.
11. **All N new ›** opens the full list on the Commands page, grouped by area.
12. The card stays quiet: it waits until the pick-up card is gone, closes by itself after 45 s (unless you're pointing at it) and shows once per update.
13. **`/whats-new`** in any chat: the latest update's picks (or the newest round's when you haven't updated), with ▶ 1…5 buttons.
14. **`/whats-new show 2`** / **`/whats-new skip 2`**: Show me / Not for me from the chat.
15. **`/whats-new round 14`**: any round's picks.
16. **`/whats-new history`**: every round with what it brought and how many upgrades, plus your last updates (from → to, how many new).
17. **`/whats-new all`**: the full list on the Commands page.
18. **History by round** on the page: a Latest / Round N picker over the list.
19. **Show all N** on the page: everything new, not just the picks.
20. Ctrl/⌘+K: "What's new in Hearth".

## Guided tours (spotlights)
21. **`/tour renders`**, the render queue: where ⇪ shows, the queue (it opens), presets in one panel, from a chat.
22. **`/tour studio`**, chaining things: `;` and `|` in a chat or the command bar, "then another command" on ☰ Commands, Studio pipelines (once Studio is in), `/dispatch`.
23. **`/tour eyes`**, board eyes and Pinterest: the board, Show to a chat, Pinterest with ＋ Board, `/board-tools on` → "look at my board".
24. **`/tour apps`**, Apps and Meshy: the Apps picker, ✨ Discover, `/meshy` (cost shown, asks first), where downloads go.
25. **`/tour access`**, chat access cards: ⋯ → Access, `/access`, the Allow once / for this chat / Deny card, Trash, Undo and the log.
26. **`/tour sequence`**, Lab sequence editing: ▤ Sequence, nudge / trim keys, right-click a clip, ✦ Arrange, ⇪ Render and `/sequence health`.
27. **`/tour keys`**, keys and Again: the keys button, Ctrl/⌘+. Again, Alt / Ctrl to reveal, one-key routines.
28. **`/tour discover`**: What's new, the feature map, ideas.
29. **A ring on the real control** and one small card next to it: the step's text, ‹ Back, Next ›, and **Try …** to run the step's command (or put it in the chat box).
30. **It opens the place for you** (the Lab, the board, the Commands page, the render queue) before pointing at it.
31. **It never blocks your work**: there's no dark overlay and the app keeps working underneath. Drag the card by its title to move it.
32. **× or Esc pauses it**, and **`/tour renders`** later picks up at the same step. **`/spotlight restart renders`** starts over.
33. If a control isn't on screen, the card says so and the tour carries on.
34. **`/spotlight`** lists every tour with its progress (paused at 2/4, seen ✓). **`/spotlight stop`** pauses the running one.
35. **`/tour` is shared fairly**: tour names start a spotlight, and everything else (`/tour intro`, `/tour hello`, `/tour new`…) is still your scripted capture tours.
36. Ctrl/⌘+K: "Guided tour of a new feature…" opens a menu of the tours.

## One tip a day
37. **"Did you know?"**: at most one small card a day, about something you have **never used**, matched to where you are (the Lab, a chat, the board, Video Review).
38. 23 tips, for example: hold 🎲 Shuffle for thumbnails, Ctrl/⌘+. Again, `/jam`, ▤ Sequence, `/arrange`, `/decide look`, `/kinetic`, `/camera-move`, `/footage`, `/meshy`, `/renders`, `/resume`, the command bar, `/board look`, `/access`, `/dispatch`, `/makes`, Pinterest, `/vibe`, `/transition`, `/add-title`.
39. **Try it** runs it (or puts it in the chat box); **Show me around** starts its tour when there is one.
40. **Later**: you'll see that tip again in three days at the earliest.
41. **Stop tips** turns tips off for good; **`/tip on`** brings them back.
42. Tips stay quiet: none in the first two minutes after Hearth opens, none over another card, none during a tour, and none while Hearth is hidden.
43. Once you've used something, its tip is gone. A tip you've seen doesn't come back.
44. **`/tip`** shows one now; **`/tip off`** / **`/tip on`**.

## Feature map and Surprise me
45. **☰ Commands › ✦ Discover** is one row at the top of the Commands page. It has four tabs: What's new · Feature map · Ideas for tonight · Guided tours.
46. When you search the Commands page ("ideas", "new", "tour"…), the four parts show as their own rows.
47. **The feature map**: every area (Video, Three.js Lab, Board, App…) with a bar of what you have used and a count of what you haven't tried.
48. Point at an area to see what you've never tried there; click it to list its commands.
49. **"You have tried N of M commands"** sits at the top.
50. **✦ Surprise me** picks something fun you've never tried, preferring things that work where you are. It runs it when that's safe, otherwise it puts it in the chat box.
51. Surprise me doesn't repeat itself (it remembers its last six picks).
52. **`/feature-map`** in a chat: the map as a short list with a suggestion per area, plus ✦ Surprise me and Open the map buttons.
53. **`/surprise-me`** from any chat.
54. Ctrl/⌘+K: "Feature map: what you never tried", "Surprise me: try something new".

## Under the hood
Not counted above: `Tour.pulse(control)` is the same pulse ring on its own, for other features to point at
something. ⬆ Update saves the list of upgrade notes it changed and announces the update when Hearth comes back
(`update.last`, `hearth:updated`). The check is `sh dev/run-checks.sh tour-discover`: a faked update, one tour, the
tip limit, Surprise me and ideas.
