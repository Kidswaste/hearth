# Round 29: safer, faster, Chrome sign-in
*October 2026*

## 🛟 Never lose work
If Hearth crashes or is killed, your unsaved work comes back.
**Find it:** by itself, on the next start; again any time from Ctrl/⌘+K › "What came back".
- Lab code, unsaved sliders, chat drafts, board edits and half-written text are kept as you go.
- A calm card lists what came back, with Open buttons. Newer work is never overwritten.
- If the window's page crashes, it reloads itself.

## ✦ Make it from my words
Describe a scene; get 3 picks instantly, with no tokens.
**Find it:** the gallery's search, a new scene's "✦ Describe it" chip, ✦ More › Look & motion, the Lab mission.
- Colors, speed, intensity, look and what follows the music are set from your words.
- "✦ Ask the director to take it further" is optional, cost shown first.

## 🎞 Solid sequences
The Lab timeline no longer goes black or glitches when scenes swap.
- Scrubbing across cuts, re-running a scene while it plays, quick scene switches: fixed (7 bugs).
- A scene that breaks is marked on its own clip, with the error first in its menu.

## 🌐 Google sign-in through Chrome
Google refuses sign-ins inside embedded apps; now Hearth uses your real Chrome.
**Find it:** the "Sign in with Chrome instead" bar on Google's error page; an app window's ⋯ › Open in Chrome; ⋯ AI setup › What it can use › Chrome.
- Hearth has its own Chrome profile: sign in once there; the AIs can still read, click and type in those apps.
- Gemini, NotebookLM and other Google-only sites open in Chrome by themselves.

## ↔ Resizable chats
**Find it:** drag the gap between chats in the grid view (double-click = equal); drag a docked chat's divider (now up to 80% of the window).

## 🐞 Bug fixes
- The Lab could open twice in one window (double the graphics work, keys going to the hidden copy): fixed. That was also why undoing marked kicks with Ctrl+Z sometimes did nothing.
- Switching scenes in a chat cross-fades from a real picture again, even when the computer is busy.
- Sync: a file saved in the same millisecond as a sync pass could wait a minute longer than it should: fixed.

## ⚡ Faster start
- The window paints about 40% sooner on reload; the Lab loads right after the first frame instead of before it.

## 📰 Patch notes
These pages. **Find it:** the ⬆ Update dialog, the note after an update, Settings › Updates › Patch notes…, Ctrl/⌘+K.

## Good to know
- A real Google account and real Mac / Windows Chrome weren't tried yet: your first Chrome sign-in is the real test.
- The Lab's very first picture isn't faster (that's the graphics card warming up).
