# Round 43: your data is safe everywhere, Try it first, Cost coach, graphics recovery, calmer menus
*October 2026*

## 🛡 Your work is safe everywhere
After the Lab scenes scare, the same protection now covers everything Hearth keeps: chats, memory, settings, notes, prompts, tags, boards, projects and tool data.
- A file held for a moment (antivirus, OneDrive, iCloud — mostly on Windows) is never read as "empty" and saved over. Hearth retries, uses the previous copy, and never writes a file it couldn't read.
- If something can't be read at start, a calm card says what — and that nothing was changed.
- **Settings are never replaced by a fresh default**; a chat that can't be read stays in your list; sync never treats a folder it couldn't look at as "everything deleted".
- Automatic copies of your settings, memory and other important files.
- **🛟 Recover…** (Settings › Backups, Ctrl/⌘+K) finds lost chats, memory, settings and Lab scenes in copies, sync's set-asides and backups — and only ever adds them next to yours.

## 🎬 Try it first (Lab director)
- The director's edit shows in the preview but isn't saved until you say so: **Keep** · **Undo** · **Try another way** (≈ 50 tokens, so it tries something different).
- Turn on in the director chat's ⋯ AI setup › Habits, or ◎ **Show me first** for one message. Off by default.

## 🧭 Cost coach
- ⋯ AI setup › Spending › Cost coach: where your tokens went this week (by AI, kind of chat, cause) and the 3 changes that would have saved the most — each one click on a setting you already have, with Undo.
- A calm note once a week, only when one change would save at least a tenth of your week. 0 tokens.

## 🎮 Graphics card resets
- If the graphics card resets (driver update, wake from sleep, a new monitor), the Lab picks up where it was: sliders, music position, camera, undo history.
- Nothing is saved while it recovers; a render carries on from the frame it was on.
- Drawing without the graphics card? One note offers **Restart**.

## 📋 Calmer menus
- The Lab picture's right-click: 23 rows → 11, most used first (♪ Music is right there now). Toolbar ⋯: 17 → 11. Sketch ▾ hides nothing behind "This sketch".
- Submenus inside submenus open on hover; the same action has the same name and icon everywhere; no command names in menus. Nothing removed.

## Good to know
- After a failed read, a delete in that store only sticks once the window reloads — Hearth errs toward keeping your things.
- Cost coach's savings are estimates in tokens (the cache makes re-reads cheaper in money).
- "📷 Camera" in the Lab's menus is now "📷 Webcam".
