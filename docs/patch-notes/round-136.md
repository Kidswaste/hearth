# Round 136: a tidier layer list, and your visuals on your phone
*November 2026*

## 📁 A tidier layer list
- The Layers list has a small **⋯** next to ＋ Layer: **🔍 Find a layer** (or Ctrl/⌘+F in the list), **✦ Name these for
  me** (plain names like "Glow ring" or "Kick particles", worked out on your computer), **✦ Name them with AI…** (cost
  shown first), group the picked layers, fold or unfold every group, show every layer.
- **Double-click** a layer's or a group's name to rename it. **Alt+↑ / Alt+↓** moves the selected layer, or a whole
  group after clicking its header.
- Right-click a group: a **colour tag**, and **Fade, move & key…** to fade, move and key the whole group at once.
  Groups can still be muted or soloed, and renders follow.
- Every change is one Undo (Ctrl/⌘+Z). The AI sees your groups too, so "make the bass group calmer" works.

## 📱 See it on your phone
- When a render finishes, **📱 Phone** on its note (or right-click its row in the render queue › Open on my phone);
  for the live preview, right-click **▣ Present** › **📱 Live on my phone**. Scan the QR code with your phone: the
  video plays full screen and looping (🔇 to hear it), or the preview plays live while the song plays on the computer.
- It stays on your own Wi-Fi: nothing goes on the internet, only the QR code's link opens it, and it stops when you
  click ■ Stop, close the card, or after 30 minutes with nobody watching. On Windows, the first time, tick "Private
  networks" and click Allow.
