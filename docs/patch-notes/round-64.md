# Round 64: everything by clicking, the Paris map that shows it all, Serum 2 / Vital inside Hearth
*October 2026*

## 🗺 The Paris map shows everything — no commands
- Open Sidequest Studio › the Paris map: the guide's places, this week's shows (with venue logos) and what you've already done are there straight away. One row of chips — **Places · Shows · My walks · Visited** — shows or hides each (Hearth remembers). **Plan a walk** is a button on the map.
- Tap a place: its quest steps and **Start this quest**. Tap a show's venue: **Make it a quest · Get ready · Keep in my lineup · Tickets**. If Shotgun / DICE can't be reached, a small line says so (click to retry).
- One pin per place: a place you pinned yourself keeps its pin and gets the guide's quest; pins that were doubled up are merged once, keeping your visits, stars and quests.

## 🖱 Every new feature is a click away
- The ✦ chip's panel: **Plan your week**, **Week in review**, **Make a walk**, rewards, and **⋯ More** (shows, My month in Paris…). Sidequest Studio has a **My week ▾** menu.
- The Sound desk's top row: **🌳 Park mode**, **Arrange loops**, **Sound bank**, **🎤 Live set**, **🎚 Check my audio setup**.
- ▶ Play's right-click: Playtest log and hitch map; while playing, press F for **✎ Note** and **● Record hitch map**.
- **✦ Try what's new** (What's new › Try it, or "✦ New" in the tracker panel): a 30–60 s hands-on tour of each of the 13 things from rounds 61–63.

## 🎹 Serum 2 / Vital
- **Their real window inside Hearth** (Windows): the plugin's own editor sits in the Sound desk; **Pop out** if you prefer it separate.
- **48 kHz** everywhere: live, previews, park takes, exports.
- **Much faster to load**: first sound in a fraction of a second instead of several seconds; all controls ready almost at once from the second time.
- **The ✦ Preset window tweaks silently**: "Darker and slower — applied" with undo / redo, and the knobs move in the plugin's own window.
- **🎚 Check my audio setup**: one card that checks your runtime, ASIO and UA interface, MIDI, Serum 2 / Vital, a 2-second live test, preset folders and ffmpeg — each with a fix button, and **Copy report**.

## 🎤 Live set for an open mic
- Big pads for your songs, park loops and presets: loops start on the bar, presets switch on the beat with no gap, a filter knob per pad, a panic stop (Esc), a full-screen Stage mode, and Rehearse to record the run as proof for your open-mic quest.

## ✓ My Paris
- What you actually did, on the map: places, shows, walks and park jams where they happened, a month strip, and **My month in Paris** (counts, arrondissements lighting up, how much of Paris you've explored) with a picture card you can save.

## 📊 Frame budget bar in the Lab
- A thin bar after the fps shows whether your scene fits a 240 Hz frame. Click it for each layer's cost; **Why?** explains the most expensive layer with plain suggestions you can send to the Three Director.

## 🛡 One Hearth at a time
- A second copy of Hearth now keeps its own settings and, if you already have Hearth with your chats, offers to open that one (or bring its chats over) instead of starting empty.
