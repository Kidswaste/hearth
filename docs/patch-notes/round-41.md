# Round 41: 🔁 Keep going, one Undo everywhere, faster renders, lighter tools, smoother
*October 2026*

## 🔁 Keep going
A chat keeps improving its own work after each reply — for a time, a number of extra rounds, or until you stop it.
**Find it:** ◎ › More › 🔁 Keep going… (your message goes first), a reply's ⋯ › Reply › Keep going from here…, or Ctrl/⌘+K.
- Choose **what it asks each time** (prefilled: "do the next 5 improvements, then summarise"), **how long** (30 min · 1 h · 3 h · until a time · N more rounds · until I stop it) and **the pause between rounds** (none · 1 min · until you're idle 10 min · when you haven't written for an hour).
- Each round is marked 🔁 with its cost; the chat header shows "🔁 3 of 5 · ≈ 42k tokens so far" with ❚❚ and ■.
- Long and "until I stop it" routines need a spending cap (your weekly limit by default) and stop politely before it.
- It also stops on the same error twice, at usage limits, or when the AI says it's done or stuck. It never answers a permission card for you — it pauses and asks. Writing in the chat pauses it.
- Works with Claude, Astra and the Lab director. After a restart it asks before resuming.

## ↶ One Undo everywhere
- **Ctrl/⌘+Z** outside a text box takes back your last change: a setting, an AI's setup, a deleted/renamed/moved chat, a memory tidy, a deleted recipe or template. **Ctrl/⌘+Shift+Z** redoes.
- **Activity › Recent changes** lists them in plain words, each with ↶.
- Text boxes, the Lab and the board keep their own undo.

## 🎞 Faster renders
- Same files, same frames — faster: 4K clips about a third faster, ▦ All formats about twice as fast (19 % less render time overall in our test).
- Videos stream into the encoder while they draw (no temporary frames on disk); "time left" counts down from the first frame.
- While a render runs, the preview eases to 30 fps so the render gets the graphics card.

## 🪙 Lighter tools on every message
- Plain Claude ≈ 90 fewer tokens per message, the Three Director ≈ 275 fewer per step, a chat with board and screen tools ≈ 550 fewer (about 30 %).
- Board, screenshot, Adobe, publishing and app tools come along only when likely needed (used this week, just switched on, or named in your message — "my board", "take a screenshot").

## 🌀 Smoother
- A waiting tip no longer re-checks the screen while you're elsewhere; Lab knobs redraw only what changed; the screen-reader pass only looks at new or changed buttons.

## Good to know
- An AI won't reach for a sleeping tool set unprompted if it hasn't used it in a week — name it in your message ("on my board") and it's back at once.
- "Until I stop it" routines always have a cap; you can raise it in the Keep going card.
- On the Mac, ⌘Z outside text boxes depends on the Edit menu letting it through (Activity › Recent changes always works).
