# Round 179: a moodboard read deeply, for inspiration and not copies
*November 2026*

## ◐ Mood read
- A moodboard can now be **read deeply**. Every picture is looked at at full size, three times: its colours, light
  and grain; its shapes, composition, depth and movement; its era, what it echoes and how it feels. A clip is read
  from six full frames. Then the board is read as a whole: what your pictures share, the different directions inside
  it, where they disagree, and what the mood is *not*.
- Where to find it: **◐ Mood read** on a project's plan card, in a moodboard's menu, or `/moodread`.
- **It tells you the cost first**, in tokens and at your own average price, and reads nothing until you say yes. It
  uses your AI's own model, not the cheap helper: this is the expensive, careful read you asked for. Rough sizes: a
  board of 34 pictures is about 260k tokens in; five clips about 180k; a board as big as INSPO about 1.9 million.
  These are estimates: the first real read will show how close they are. You
  can read only your starred or liked pictures, or only what is selected on the board.
- **You pay for a picture once.** The read is kept with the board. Add or change a picture and a refresh reads only
  that one, then the board again. You can stop a long read; what was read is kept.

## 🔎 You can check it
- Under each picture you see what the AI says it saw (one plain line, only for you), the feeling it found, and what to
  carry into new work.
- Next to that are the **numbers measured on your computer**. If the read says "warm" and the measurement says cool,
  it is flagged.
- A **second AI looks at each picture on its own** (Astra when it is set up) and the two reads sit side by side, with
  what they disagree on. The panel counts the pictures worth a look.
- **You can correct it.** Edit the whole read, add "My corrections" in your own words (they come first in what the AIs
  get, and every later refresh reads them), correct one picture, or have one read again.

## 🎨 Inspiration, not copies
- When a director reads the project brief it now gets the mood read: qualities and directions, not descriptions of
  your references. The plain line of what each picture shows and the pictures' names are never passed on.
- It comes with a clear rule: make something new in this mood; don't rebuild a reference's subject, layout or
  recognisable parts; don't put a reference's file in the scene.
- The project brief is written from the read, and no longer proposes importing a reference clip as footage.
- **"This is mine: may be used as is"**: tick it on your own footage, photos and artwork (in the mood read, or
  right-click the picture on the board). Those are the only pictures the AIs are told they may use as they are.
- If an AI tries to add a moodboard picture to a scene's references and it isn't marked as yours, Hearth refuses and
  tells it to borrow the feel instead. Files you attach yourself are not affected.
- Everything you do by hand is unchanged: moodboard fills, dropping a tile on the timeline, "How close am I?".
  "Closer" and "Close to it" now say that they move toward a copy.

## Good to know
- How good a real read is has not been judged yet: the checks use a stand-in AI. Try it on a small board first and
  tell us where it is vague or wrong.
- The full-size pictures made for a read stay in Hearth's attachments folder.
- A big board takes a long time to read; keep Hearth open while it runs.
