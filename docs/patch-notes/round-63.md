# Round 63: walks, a sound bank, songs from the park, a hitch map, real rewards
*October 2026*

## 🚶 Quest walks
- `/quest walk belleville 3h`, `/quest walk records 2h` or `/quest walk from canal`: 3–5 Paris places you haven't visited, in the best order, from Métro to Métro, fitting the time you asked — ending at tonight's show if you have one. A dotted route on the map, "Open walking route" in Google Maps, and save it as a quest the ✦ chip follows stop by stop. `/quest week` can propose a walk on a free afternoon.

## 🎛 Sound bank
- `/sounds` (or Keep a patch › Sound bank…): every Serum 2 / Vital preset you have — Hearth's bank, the ✦ Preset window's, and (if you allow it) your plugins' own user folders, read-only. Search, tags, hover to hear a 3-second preview, click to play it live, drag into a park jam, favorites and "≈ similar". Previews are made only when you ask, in the background, never touching your live sound.

## 🎶 From park loops to a song
- Arrange (in the Park jam fold, or `/song`): your park takes on 4 lanes, snapped to bars, repeat / trim / fade / volume / ambience on-off, tempo matched; play a take's notes through another preset. Export a mix, stems, the MIDI (and an MP3 with ffmpeg) into the sketch — and offer it to a new "Finish a track" quest.

## 📍 Hitch map for Clink
- `/playtest hitchmap on` (or right-click in ▶ Play): records where you were and how long each frame took. After playing: a heat map of where Clink stutters, the worst spots with pictures, "Jump there" and "Fix this" for the Three Director. Off by default; about 0.02 ms a frame when on.

## 🎁 Real rewards
- Set rewards that cost XP ("new record at Superfly, 300 XP", "concert fund +10 €, 500 XP"), see "120 XP to your next reward" in the ✦ tracker, claim with a confirm, keep a savings-jar note (Hearth never touches money), and a small weekly streak bonus. `/reward`.
