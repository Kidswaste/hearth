# Round 116: a lighter Hearth, chats inside projects, a smarter Astra
*November 2026*

## 🪶 Hearth is lighter
- Hearth is now what you use it for: chats with Claude and Astra in your projects, synced between your computers, and
  the Three.js Lab the AIs can drive.
- The **Life tab** is gone: quests, the Paris map, outings, shows, leave reminders, tickets, rewards, the week plan and
  the tours.
- The **music studio** is gone too: the Sound desk, the Serum 2 / Vital instruments, the live set, park jam, the sound
  bank, arranging loops, MIDI learn, send to DAW, the audio doctor and the playback volume button.
- **Your data is kept untouched**: nothing was deleted, moved or changed in your data folder, and sync still carries
  those files, so a computer still on the older version keeps everything. The app just doesn't open them any more.
- Everything the Lab uses for music visuals stays: songs, the beat grid, live sound input, Spotify, analysis and the
  Lab's own sound.
- Hearth carries about 2.5 MB less code (−11 %), and starts with a little less to load.

## 📁 Chats live inside projects
- The chats list on the left shows only the chats of the project you have open, with the project's name at the top:
  click it to switch project or make a new one.
- **＋ New chat** starts the chat in that project, with whichever AI you pick. If no project is open, Hearth asks which
  project the chat is for.
- Right-click a chat › **Move to project…** moves it to another project. A chat is always in exactly one project.
- Chats from before this change that weren't in a project are in a new project called **Earlier chats**: rename it or
  move chats out of it whenever you like. A chat that was in several projects now stays in one of them. Nothing was
  deleted, and your two computers sort them the same way.
- Ctrl/⌘+K still finds chats in every project.

## ✧ Astra works like Claude
- Astra now keeps its own built-in know-how: how to use its tools, edit files and plan its work. Before, Hearth's
  instructions replaced it, which is why Astra often seemed more limited than Claude for the same ask.
- Astra now knows it is running inside Hearth, like Claude.
- When you let an AI work on Hearth's own code, both Claude and Astra get a short map of how Hearth is built.
- In the Three Director, a chat you opened for the other AI with the ◆ switcher stays that AI: `/director-engine`,
  "Ask … instead" and the history labels follow that chat.
- **Try it**: in the Three Director, ask Claude "Add a slow orbiting ring of 40 small glowing spheres as a new layer,
  with sliders for speed and count", then switch to Astra with **◆ Claude ▾** (Fresh chat) and ask the same. Both
  should build a separate layer with sliders and check it before saying done.
