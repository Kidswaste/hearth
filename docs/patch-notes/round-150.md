# Round 150: a clean stage for your scene
*November 2026*

You asked for the scene view to look more mono and futuristic, react to what's playing, and keep the buttons out of the
way. Here's what changed in the Lab's preview.

## 🧹 The buttons get out of the way
- Stop moving the mouse over the picture for 2 seconds and the buttons and numbers fade away. You see just the scene
  in a thin frame.
- They come back where you point:
  - **Mouse to the top-right corner** → Freeze, Fit, 9:16, 16:9, 4:5, 1:1, More…, 📷 and ⋯.
  - **Mouse to the bottom edge** → the fps line, the Quality chip and the rest of the numbers.
  - **Mouse over the "01 PREVIEW" title** above the picture → its fps and quality readout.
  - Move anywhere over the picture and two small hints, **VIEW** (top right) and **STATS** (bottom), show where they are.
- **Press I** to keep everything showing. Press **I** again for the clean stage. You can also type "show scene
  controls" or "clean stage" in the command bar (Ctrl/⌘+;), or `/stage show` / `/stage clean`.
- Warnings still find you: a new "Slow: … · Why?" chip or a Safe open note shows by itself for a few seconds, then
  shrinks to a small glowing **!** (or 🛟) in its corner. Move the mouse and it opens again. Error notes always stay.
- Nothing was removed. Every button, menu, right-click and key works as before.

## 🛰 A mono, futuristic frame
- Thin corner brackets, measuring-tape ticks along the edges, and a hairline along the bottom showing where you are in
  the song.
- While the mouse moves: small mono labels (playing / stopped, frame size, beat lights and BPM, the time, the fps) and
  a tiny crosshair in the middle.
- The buttons look like an instrument panel now: see-through, thin outlines when you point at them, all one colour.
- The orange ember only shows up for warnings and when the picture is frozen.

## 🌈 It reacts to your scene and your music
- The frame takes on the scene's main colour: a red scene turns it red, a blue one blue, a grey one back to the Lab's
  blue. It glides between colours.
- While the song plays, the corners pop out a little on every kick, the edge lines flash on snares, the beat lights
  follow the bars and a soft glow follows how loud the song is.
- It costs very little: on our slow test computer about 3 thousandths of a second of work per second in total, far
  below one frame.
- Want it quieter? **Lab ⋯** (top right of the Lab) › **View & help** › **Stage**: each click goes **reactive → calm
  (colour only, no pulsing) → off (still blue)**.
- With calmer motion (Appearance › Motion: calm / off, or your computer's "reduce motion") nothing pulses or glides.
  The **Plain** Lab look keeps just the hiding buttons, without the frame.
- None of this is in your renders, stills or exports. It's only drawn around the preview.

## Try it
1. Open a scene with a song, press **Space**, and leave the mouse still: the stage goes clean and pulses on the kicks.
2. Move to the **top-right corner** for the frame sizes and Freeze, and to the **bottom** for the fps line.
3. Press **I** when you want everything showing while you tweak.
