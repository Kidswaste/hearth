# Round 35: Mac + PC side by side, My templates, shorter answers, smooth long replies, 10 bugs fixed
*October 2026*

## 💻 Your Mac and your PC
- **Fixed:** the folders an AI may use on your Mac were wiped whenever settings arrived from your PC (and the other way round). Each computer now keeps its own.
- A folder used on the other computer is used here too when it's at the same place (home folder, iCloud Drive, Dropbox…). Otherwise ⋯ AI setup › Files shows **Pick this folder on this computer** — nothing is granted by guess.
- **Safety:** a Mac path can no longer slip through as a folder on Windows, and Astra's Windows "without its sandbox" never reaches the Mac.
- Engines, sign-ins and Hearth's Chrome stay on each computer.
- "This computer: Mac / Windows PC" shows in Settings › Sync; a one-time note on a new computer lists what needs a click.
- Lab songs and videos from the other computer are found again by place or content; if not, a **Find this file** button.

## 📐 My templates (Lab)
Turn any scene into your own starting point, 0 tokens.
**Find it:** right-click the preview › ✦ More › 📐 My templates › Save as my template… / New from my template…
- Its words, colors, pictures, video, song and sliders become a short form with a live preview; **Create** makes the new scene (one Undo takes it back).
- Templates sync to your other computer and can be shared as one file. Or just ask the director: "a new scene from my Neon title template with the title SUMMER".

## ✂️ Shorter answers
- ⋯ AI setup › How hard it thinks › **How long its answers are**: Short · Normal · Detailed.
- Short skips preambles, recaps and repeated code — about a quarter fewer written tokens (the expensive ones). Switching keeps the prompt cache.
- Plain chats now answer directly by default. Hover a reply's tokens to see written · thinking · tool calls.

## 🌊 Smooth long replies
- Huge replies (tables, long code, lists) stream with 2–7× less work per update; code is colored as it arrives.
- No more jump when a long reply ends or its pictures load; scrolling up stops following at once.
- In long chats, scrolling up keeps your place, and jumping to an old message lands on it. Find in chat is quicker.

## 🐞 Fixed
- Fix my scene works with looking around ("Back to the scene's camera" when you looked away).
- Right-clicking a precomp layer shows ◫ Precomp › again (plus shader, particles, type, physics submenus).
- Export the grade as a .cube and the effects picker's previews work again.
- Variations no longer shows six identical "Another angle" takes.
- After Continue lighter, the meter and mission estimates show the lighter size at once.
- Check this AI says "no internet" / "busy" / "usage limit" instead of blaming your files.
- A switch that did nothing ("Shrinks long chats by itself") is gone.

## Good to know
- Saves now wait until they're on disk (a few milliseconds) — steadier everywhere.
- Still open: a screen recording of an area where nothing moves can end early; a Swarm typed while offline is sent as a normal message once you're back.
