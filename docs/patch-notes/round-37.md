# Round 37: Before / after, a leaner director, the Lab in a blink, Find anything, safe updates
*October 2026*

## ◧ Before / after (Lab)
After any change to the look (a director edit, a 🎲 variation, a 🩺 fix, a look, a slider session, undo):
- **Hold B** to see before; let go for now. **Tap B** for a split view — drag the line, double-click to swap.
- A chip says what changed ("Before: Claude's edit · 2 min ago") with **⟲ Keep before** and **✓ Keep now**.
- Also right-click the preview › View › ◧ Before / after. 0 tokens, no second 3D view.

## 🪙 A leaner Three Director
- Its turns cost about two thirds less (≈ 18.5k → 6k tokens over eight typical asks); long director chats get cheaper with every message.
- It doesn't re-read code it already has, sees your scene as a short outline plus the layer it's working on, and takes smaller screenshots (it can ask for a bigger one). `/director-shot-size medium` brings back the old size.

## ⚡ The Lab in a blink
- Your last scene is on screen about 0.1 s after you open the Lab (was ≈ 3 s); the full scene and its music follow in under a second.
- Switching scenes is about 2.5× faster; switching frame size (9:16, Fit…) no longer reloads the preview — the song keeps playing.
- Still no black flash between scenes.

## 🔎 Find anything (Ctrl/⌘+K)
- Plain words, Enter takes you there: any Settings or AI setup row (its fold opens, the row lights up), menu actions, missions, chats — even **what was said in them**.
- "dark mode" → Match my computer, "cheaper" → Spending / Short answers, "permissions" → Files it can use. In the Lab, Lab search's results show too.

## ⬆ Safe updates (Mac and Windows)
- Updating can't lose your work: hand edits are put aside and come straight back.
- Every failure gets one plain sentence and one button (Install Git, Try again, Allow long paths…). Anything needing your password runs only in a window you can see, after your click.
- Windows holding files open? The update finishes as Hearth restarts. A crash mid-update is noticed and offers Finish or Go back.
- **↶ Go back** in the "Hearth updated" note returns to the previous version; your chats and data are never touched.

## ⛶ Big screen
- **⛶ in the rail** (or F11 on Windows, ⌃⌘F on the Mac, ⌘/Ctrl+K "Full screen") makes Hearth full screen. Esc never kicks you out while typing.
- Full screen is remembered per screen: on a new monitor setup, Hearth opens full screen on the big monitor and in a window on the laptop.
- **Size of everything** (Appearance): Auto · 100–200 %, kept per screen. Auto makes a 4K at 100 % show at 150 % so text is readable from your chair. ⌘/Ctrl + / − change it, ⌘/Ctrl 0 goes back to Auto.
- In full screen with the chats list hidden, rest the pointer on the rail and the list slides in.
- The Lab drops its preview resolution sooner on a huge preview when frames get slow (renders stay full quality).

## Good to know
- Director screenshots are smaller by default; ask for "a bigger screenshot" when detail matters.
- The Lab prepares itself after start only if you used it in the last two weeks, and lets go after 15 minutes unused.
