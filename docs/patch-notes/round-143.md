# Round 143: a lighter Lab, with a Forgeheart look
*November 2026*

## ▾ Fewer little buttons
- Small buttons that go together now sit behind one labelled button. Click it: the real buttons open in a small tray
  under it. Esc or a click anywhere else closes it. Nothing was removed, and every key still works (T, K, S, H, Space…).
- **♩ Beat ▾** (timeline): Tap, K Kick, S Snare, H Hit, and the whole beat grid (BPM, Set 1 here, ◂ ▸, RGB). The BPM
  still shows beside it and flashes on the beat. The grid no longer takes a row of its own, so the timeline is taller.
- **⚡ Sync ▾** (timeline): 🎧 Live, ⚡ Triggers, the style preset (Techno ▾…) and Auto bars. A green dot shows while
  live sound is on.
- **View ▾** and **Sections ▾** moved up into the timeline's top row.
- **⚙ Inspect ▾** (Lab bar): ›_ Console and ✥ Edit. Sliders, Code, Present and Play stay where they were.
- **Right-click** one of these buttons: the same controls as a list (arrow keys and Enter work).

## 🖱 More right-click menus
- **A layer's bar on the timeline:** mute, solo, its curves, play it during the loop or the whole song, start / end at
  the playhead, and "More for this layer…".
- **The time ruler:** **Set 1 here** and **Zoom ›** (in, out, the loop, the whole song).
- **The picture:** **View › Quality ›** (Smooth · Balanced · Force high quality) and **Capture › ⇪ Render this…**.
- **A layer in Layers & knobs:** right-click › More… › show / fold its knobs, 📌 keep them open.
- The timeline's tracks now say "▾ curves", dimmed, instead of "▾ Automation" on every line.

## ✦ Forgeheart look
- Faint scanlines on the Lab's panels (never on your picture), an ember-to-violet edge, a slow glint gliding along the
  timeline and the Layers & knobs title, and an ember playhead that gently glows while the song is stopped.
- It all holds still while the scene plays, so playback stays smooth, and it's off if you turned motion down.
- Prefer it flat? **⋯ (Lab bar) › View & help › Lab look: Forgeheart** switches it to Plain (click again to bring it back).
