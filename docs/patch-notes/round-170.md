# Round 170: switching chats draws lighter
*November 2026*

## Switching chats and projects

- **Switching to another chat or project now costs the graphics side about 20 % less in the plain looks and about
  40 % less in Forgeheart.** The chat still slides in the same way; it just no longer redraws the whole chat behind
  the scenes while it does. It looks the same.

## Esc closes AI setup again

- **Esc closes the AI setup drawer again** after you've used ★ Favourites or 🎛 Dials in the Lab: those hidden Lab
  panels were catching the key.
