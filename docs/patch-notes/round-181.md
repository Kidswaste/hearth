# Round 181: the mood read works by itself
*November 2026*

## ◐ New pictures are read as they land
- **Add a picture or a clip to a moodboard and it is read a few seconds later**, without a click and without the cost
  question. Then the whole-board mood is worked out again with it.
- It costs about 4 AI passes a picture (a clip: six full frames in each), plus one pass over the board. A small note
  says when a read starts and what it cost when it ends.
- Changing "What I like about this" or a picture's note reads that picture again. Moving a picture reads nothing.
- **Only what you just added.** A board full of older pictures that were never read is still your click (◐ Mood read),
  with the cost shown first. Hearth tells you once when a board has older pictures outside the read.
- The switch is in the ◐ Mood read panel: **"Read new pictures by themselves, as they land"**, on by default.

## 🎨 The mood goes into every message
- **A director's chat in a project now gets the moodboard's mood with every message**: the mood line, your own
  corrections, how to make new work in it, what it is not, and the inspiration rule. Before, the AI only got it when it
  chose to open the project brief.
- It is the short form (up to 2000 characters). The whole read is still there when the AI opens the brief.
- Still no descriptions of your references and none of their names.

## Good to know
- This is for chats that belong to a project with a moodboard. A chat outside a project does not get the mood by
  itself yet.
- The project brief is not rewritten by itself when the mood changes: it shows "the mood read changed" until you
  refresh it.
- How good a real read is has still not been judged: the checks use a stand-in AI.
