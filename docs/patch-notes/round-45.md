# Round 45: text that moves, automatic backups, plain errors, free answers, instant chat switching
*October 2026*

## 🅰 Text that moves with the music
- Right-click the Lab preview › ＋ Add › **🅰 Text that moves…**: 22 presets with live previews (word by word pop, letter cascade, scale punch on the kick, countdown, neon flicker…).
- Pick what it moves on (beat, bar, kick, snare or seconds), stagger, intensity, easing, font, a palette color, outline and shadow.
- **Lyric lines**: paste them, then tap along (Space at each line), put them on your markers, or spread them on the song.
- Renders match the preview frame for frame. Saved with scenes, templates (words stay editable), recipes and packs. Every change is one undo.
- Also fixed: `/text set`, `/lyrics` and tap-along work again.

## 💾 Hearth backs up by itself
- Once a day when the computer is idle, and before every update and restore — never while you render or record, never on low battery.
- **Every backup is checked** after it's written; one that fails isn't kept, and Hearth says why with Retry.
- Settings › This computer & sync: "Last backup: 2 h ago ✓". **Backups…** lists them with what's inside, **Restore missing things** (only adds — you see the list first) and **Show in Finder / Explorer**.
- Each computer picks its own folder (Documents › Hearth backups to start; iCloud Drive or OneDrive offered).
- Kept: the last 7 days, 4 weeks and 6 months. Big videos are saved once, not every day.

## 🙂 Errors in plain words
- One sentence that says what happened and what to do — never "ENOENT", stack traces or JSON.
- The fix is a button where there is one: Show folder, Retry, the right Settings row, Check this AI, 🩺 Fix my scene.
- What the computer actually said is behind a small **▸ Details** (with Copy).
- The Lab console gets one plain line for your scene's error; the raw lines stay below for code work.

## 💡 Answered by Hearth
- Ask a chat about Hearth itself ("how do I make a 9:16 version?", "where's the spending limit?") and a small card answers right away, for free — nothing sent.
- **Show me** takes you there. **Ask the AI anyway** sends it as usual.
- Code, scenes, writing and anything it isn't sure about go straight to the AI. Turn off in ⋯ AI setup › Habits.

## ⚡ Instant chat switching
- Going back to a chat you just looked at is instant (about 40× less work for a chat full of code).
- A chat you open for the first time shows its latest messages straight away; earlier ones slip in above.
- Resting the pointer on a chat in the list gets it ready before you click.
- Fixed: jumping to an old message no longer sometimes jumps in the next chat you open.
