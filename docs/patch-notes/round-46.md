# Round 46: move things on the preview, keyframes you can shape, fewer tokens, smoother, ready for Mac and PC
*October 2026*

## ✥ Work on the picture itself
- Click something in the Lab preview to pick its layer (the Layers list follows), then drag it where you want it.
- Shift while dragging snaps to the centre, thirds, edges or other layers (a thin guide shows).
- Drag a corner to scale, the little circle to turn, or Alt/Option+scroll; arrow keys nudge (Shift: 10×). Esc lets go.
- Double-click something to open its sliders. Each drag is one Ctrl/⌘+Z.
- Renders, the sequence, templates and recipes match what you see. 3D layers show **✥ 3D** for the 3D handles.

## ◆ Keyframes you can see and shape
- A slider's keyframes show as diamonds; pressing its ◇ opens the lane.
- Click a diamond: **Linear, Ease, Ease in, Ease out, Bounce, Hold** — or **Curve**, with two handles to drag. Renders match the preview exactly.
- Shift+click diamonds (or Shift+drag a box) and drag them together, on the beat. **Alt+drag copies**; Ctrl/⌘+C / V pastes at the playhead.
- **Snap to beats** while a song is loaded. Every change is one undo.
- Note: stretching lane points is now **Shift+Alt+drag** (Alt+drag copies).

## 🪶 Fewer tokens on every message
- The fixed part of every message is smaller: plain chats ≈ 11 %, the Three Director ≈ 14 %, Forge ≈ 23 %, 🔁 Keep going ≈ 14 %.
- Very long tool results come in parts (the AI asks for more only if it needs it); Forge screenshots sent to the AI are smaller.
- Sleeping tool sets now wake only on your own words, not on a saved note that happens to mention them.

## ⚡ Smoother
- Lab play and slider drags use about half the script time they did; menus, right-clicks, the chat list search and bursts of error notes do less work.
- Nothing looks different.

## 🍎🪟 Ready for your Mac and PC
- Backups are now zipped by Hearth itself, the same on both: Mac backups are no longer marked "damaged" by hidden `._` files, and Windows keeps accented file names.
- Old backups on a drive with no Recycle Bin (USB stick, network share) are never deleted for good.
- iCloud / OneDrive online-only files no longer make good backups look bad, and errors name them.
- On the Mac, changed files with accents in their names are noticed.
- 💡 answers say ⌘K on the Mac and Ctrl+K on Windows.

## Try it on your computers
1. Settings › This computer & sync › Backups… › **Back up now** (allow Documents / Controlled folder access if asked) — the row turns ✓.
2. Ask a chat something slow and switch apps — a notification brings you back to that chat (Windows: run "Create Start menu & desktop shortcuts" once first).
3. In the Lab, click something in the preview and drag it; click a keyframe diamond and pick Bounce.
