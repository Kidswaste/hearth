# Round 33: all formats at once, cheaper missions, Mac fixes, steady Lab, calmer AI setup
*October 2026*

## ▦ All formats (Lab)
Export your scene as 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 and 4:5 in one go — 0 tokens, nothing uploaded.
**Find it:** right-click the preview › ⇪ Export › ▦ All formats, ✦ More › ⇪ Make, the Lab toolbar's ⋯, or 📮 Ready to post.
- Each shape shows a preview first; if the subject would fall out of a vertical or square frame, Hearth reframes it (clear of the apps' buttons).
- Drag a preview to move it, scroll to zoom, ↺ to reset. Framing is remembered per scene.
- The videos render one after another in the background, into one folder, named like "My scene 9x16 1080x1920.mp4".

## 🪙 Missions cost less
- Swarm helpers and the reviewer use the lighter model and think less; the AI writing your final answer keeps its full model.
- Each costly mission card in ◎ says what it will take ("≈ 2 helpers · about 4k tokens"). Over your weekly limit? It turns orange and asks first.
- After a mission, your next message carries a short summary instead of every full answer (about half the tokens).
- Helpers get only the task and your instructions, not your memory.

## 🍎 Mac
- Claude Code / Codex are found wherever you installed them (Homebrew, npm, nvm, asdf, mise, Volta, the apps…), even when Hearth starts from the Dock.
- macOS blocking a download ("cannot be verified", "is damaged") gets one sentence and an **Allow it…** button that shows the command first. Never a password.
- Other Mac errors read as one plain sentence with the fixing button.
- Deletes stay recoverable: files on a network share or read-only disk are left in place, and the chat says why.

## 🎞 Steady Lab on fast screens
- Scenes move at the same speed on 60, 120 or 240 Hz screens.
- A still scene stops drawing (about 90% less GPU work) and wakes the instant anything changes. A hidden Lab truly sleeps.
- On battery the preview goes a bit lighter by itself; plugged in, full again. Renders are always full quality.
- New: ⋯ › Frame pace ("120 fps · steady"), off by default.

## ❖ Calmer AI setup
- Reads top to bottom: who → Personality → Instructions → What it can use → How hard it thinks. Memory, Habits, Spending and Missions wait folded at the end, each with a one-line summary.
- **What it can use** is the one place to give an AI more reach: Files, Hearth's tools, Connections, Hooks, Check this AI.
- 48 controls → 18, one screen tall. Nothing removed.
- Plain words: "Files it can use", "Everything it can do…", "Your own connections". Settings › Updates has one **What's new…** button.

## Good to know
- Mac and Windows fixes were tested on simulated machines: your next start is the real test. On the Mac, start from the Dock and run /doctor.
- All formats reframes 3D cameras; flat 2D layers and full-screen shaders keep their own layout.
- Scenes you already made keep their own speed; ask the director to make one "frame-rate independent" if it spins too fast.
