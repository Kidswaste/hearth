# Round 158: the Lab opens lighter
*November 2026*

## What changed

- **The Lab's main file is a quarter smaller.** The parts you use now and then (the references and palette tools,
  notes, MIDI, live sound, contact sheets and stills, the scene editor, the Tools drawer, the keys and history dialogs,
  the right-click menus) now load the first time you use them, not every time the Lab opens: **524 KB → 399 KB**, and
  the time your computer spends compiling the sketch editor on each open drops by about a quarter (**24 → 18 ms** on our
  test machine; a few milliseconds on yours).
- Opening the Lab, playing a scene, picking layers, moving sliders and everything the AI does on the scene work exactly
  as before, in the Lab and in the Lab window; a feature you open for the first time loads in a blink, then it's instant.
