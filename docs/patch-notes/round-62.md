# Round 62: a week of quests, music in the park, show nights, playtest notes
*October 2026*

## 🗓 Plan your week
- `/quest week` (or right-click the ✦ chip › Plan my week…) proposes 3–5 quests for the coming week: this week's shows, unvisited Paris places near that night's venue, your open personal quests, and a park music session on the best dry day. Drag them onto days; on a planned day the ✦ chip shows "Today's quest".
- An optional gentle nudge (off until you tick it, at most once a day, quiet when Do Not Disturb is on). On Sunday evenings, one offer to plan the next week.
- `/quest review`: quests done, XP, your streak of weeks out, the moments you collected — save it as a page in Sidequest Studio.

## 🌳 Park jam
- **Park mode** (the 🌳 button in the Sound desk, or `/jam park buttes`): the laptop's own output when the UA interface isn't plugged in, a safe buffer, battery-friendly settings, remembered per park; off puts everything back.
- Record a 4 / 8 / 16-bar loop of what you play (starting on your first note), with 10 seconds of park ambience from the mic underneath. Saved in the park sketch, and "Offer as park-quest proof" submits it to your park quest.

## 🎟 Show night companion
- **Get ready**: doors and start in Paris time, a calendar file / Google Calendar link, the nearest Métro stations, Citymapper / RATP links, a listen link per artist, and an optional reminder on the day.
- **How was it?**: a rating, one moment and a photo go into **Shows I went to** (venues you've been to get a ✓ on the map) and count as the show quest's proof. One tap to follow the artist or venue.
- `/shows prep <show>`, `/shows went <show>: 4/5 great encore`, `/shows log`.

## 🎮 Playtest notes for Clink
- In ▶ Play press **F8** to drop a note: a picture of that moment, the time, the game's state, the player position and the frame rate (Shift+F8 adds typed or spoken words). Back in the Lab, "3 notes from this playtest": click one to return to that moment, or **Fix this** to draft it for the Three Director. Costs nothing until you press the key. `/playtest notes`.
