# Round 40: camera moves, lighter attachments, a faster start, a calmer chat, keyboard & screen readers
*October 2026*

## 🎥 Camera moves (Lab)
Right-click the preview › 🎥 Camera moves — 0 tokens.
- Push in, pull out, orbit, dolly zoom, crane, a subtle handheld shake, whip pan, slow drift, or a **punch-in on each kick, beat or bar**.
- **From this view to that view:** look around to a start, set it, look around to an end, set it — the camera travels between them.
- Each move is a cyan bar under the waveform: drag its ends (they land on the beat). Easing and intensity in **🎥 Your moves…**; one Undo per change.
- Renders move exactly like the preview; moves are saved with the scene, templates and packs, and 📜 recipes record them.

## 📎 Lighter attachments
- Each attachment chip shows its cost before you send ("≈ 1.3k of 37k tok").
- Big files, logs and long pastes go as an outline plus the parts your message is about; the AI can open the rest. A 2,000-line file: ≈ 36k → 2k tokens.
- Pictures go at 1568 px (your original stays as it is).
- The same file again is one line; if you changed it, only the changed lines go.
- Click the cost on a chip to send that one whole. Text files up to 64 MB can be attached now.

## ⚡ A faster start
- The first picture of the window comes about 10 % sooner; ten parts the first screen doesn't need now load right after it.
- No more light/dark flicker while starting. A check keeps the first screen within budget.

## 💬 A calmer chat
- Under a reply: what it is; Copy and ⋯ when you point at it — always in the same order.
- A stopped reply shows ▶ Continue right away; a failed one shows Retry (the rest is in its ⋯).
- Several quiet notes under a reply fold into one line.
- Ctrl/⌘+K finds every message action.

## ⌨️ Keyboard & screen readers (Mac and Windows)
- Everything works from the keyboard: a clear focus ring in every look, "Skip to the chat" first, Esc closes what's on top and puts you back.
- Arrow keys in menus and the chats list.
- VoiceOver and Narrator read real names ("Mission for your next message", not "◎"); a finished reply is read once, never word by word.
- Readable contrast in the light looks; Windows High Contrast supported.

## 🐞 Fixed
- The Windows battery-saver check no longer tries to run PowerShell on other systems.

## Good to know

- A picture's fine detail may need "send whole" (click the chip's cost).
