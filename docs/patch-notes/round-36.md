# Round 36: Lab search, where tokens go, native Mac & Windows, one smooth rhythm, two bugs fixed
*October 2026*

## 🔎 Lab search
Press **/** in the Lab (or the small 🔎 next to ⋯) and type in plain words.
- One list finds menu actions, effects and looks (with pictures), your layers and sliders, My templates, gallery starting points, recent scenes and short "how do I…" help.
- **Enter does it.** "glow" finds bloom, "vertical video" finds All formats, typos are fine.
- Nothing found? Your words go into the director's box (not sent).

## 🪙 Where its tokens go
**Find it:** ⋯ AI setup › Spending › Where its tokens go.
- What every message to that AI carries before your words, line by line: instructions, personality, memory, each tool.
- Tools show how often they were used this week; one unused for 30 days gets **Turn off** (with what it saves, and Undo).
- Long instructions or memory get **Shorten**, which opens them for you to edit.

## 🍎🪟 Native on Mac and Windows
- The window reopens where it was on each computer and never off-screen (unplug a monitor and it comes back).
- Mac: no title bar — the traffic lights sit in the rail, headers move the window; full app menus (⌘, Settings, ⌘Q…). `/titlebar native` brings the old bar back.
- Windows: a clean Alt menu bar; Ctrl+W / Ctrl+R no longer close or reload the window by accident; slim scrollbars.
- ◐ **Match my computer** (Appearance): the look follows your system's light/dark mode.
- A reply that finishes while you're elsewhere badges the Dock / flashes the taskbar.
- Crisp when moving to a screen with another scale.

## 🌀 One smooth rhythm
- Menus, drawers, folds, popovers and toasts all move on the same short timing, GPU-only. AI setup slides in, ◎ rises from the chat box.
- Reduce motion in your system settings → things just fade. Closing stays instant.
- About half the styling work while opening and closing things.

## 🐞 Fixed
- Screen recordings of a still area keep their full length (a 7 s take used to come out 1 s long).
- A mission typed offline (Swarm, Ask everyone, Plan first…) waits with its mission and runs as that mission when you're back, even after a restart.
- A mission picked for a message waiting behind a running reply stays with that message.

## Good to know
- / in the Lab now opens Lab search (in the sliders column it still finds a slider).
- The Mac title bar change and the Windows taskbar flash were tested on simulated machines: your next start is the real test.
- claude.ai connector sizes in "Where its tokens go" are estimates.
