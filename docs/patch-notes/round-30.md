# Round 30: song parts, spending, recall, Pinterest, polish
*October 2026*

## 🥁 React to the drums, the bass, the voice
Hearth splits your song into parts (locally, no AI) so each can drive the visuals.
**Find it:** the ♪ panel on any slider ("The drums", "The bass", "The voice"), the presets, the Lab mission.
- Four presets: pump with the drums, throb with the bass, glow with the voice, drift with the rest.
- Renders match the preview. The split takes a few seconds once per song.

## 💰 Spending guard
**Find it:** ⋯ AI setup › Spending.
- This week's use per AI in plain words, an optional weekly limit, and what to do near it: warn me, use cheaper answers, or ask before each message.
- "This week at a glance": the biggest chats and tools, with one-click savings.
- Things that show a cost first also say when they'd pass your limit. Nothing is ever blocked.

## 🔎 What did we decide?
Search every chat for decisions and open questions, with no tokens.
**Find it:** type a question in the chats panel's search › 🔎 Find the answer; ◎ Missions › More › Remind me what we decided.
- Short quotes with the chat and date, and "Jump to it ↗".
- An AI sum-up is optional (cost first) and sends only the quotes.

## 📌 Find images like this
Pinterest (and Unsplash, Are.na, Cosmos) into your mood board.
**Find it:** the board's ⋯ › Find images like this…; right-click selected pictures; the mission "Find images for this vibe".
- Searches from the board's vibe or your words; you pick, the picks are saved with their links.
- AIs with board tools can suggest pins for you to confirm.

## ✨ Make it look finished
One click checks and fixes a Lab scene, with no tokens.
**Find it:** right-click the preview; ✦ More › Look & motion; the mission "Polish the look".
- Exposure, crushed blacks, blown whites, glow, clashing colors, flat contrast, content under the 9:16 buttons, motion, music reaction, frame rate.
- One undo step, each fix can be unticked; before → after numbers and pictures.

## 🫥 Chrome out of sight
Hearth's Chrome now runs hidden; its pages show and work inside Hearth's own windows.
**Find it:** by itself for anything that opens in Chrome; ⋯ AI setup › What it can use › Chrome › "Show Chrome" to see it anyway.
- Click, type, scroll, paste, popups, downloads (to Downloads/Hearth/<App>) and file pickers all work inside Hearth.
- Real Chrome only comes up to sign in, then steps back out of sight by itself.
- Nothing is drawn while you can't see the page.

## 🐞 Fixes
- The "Find images like this" window no longer turns into a tilted overlay across the app while it searches.
- Pictures without a title get a readable name instead of a long code.
- The screen-capture keys (Ctrl/⌘+Alt+S, A, R, V) work again from the chat box (the faster start had unhooked them).

## Good to know
- The song split is an approximation (a plucky bass can nudge "the drums", an 808 can count as bass).
- Pinterest reading wasn't tried against the real site here; if its page changes, the picker says it found nothing rather than showing wrong pictures.
- Make it look finished judges numbers, not taste: untick what you don't want.
- On a Mac, Chrome's Dock icon stays while it runs (it can't be hidden); on Windows one Chrome button stays on the taskbar.
