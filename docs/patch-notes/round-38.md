# Round 38: move with the music, tidy memory, drag & drop, calmer tips, a lighter touch
*October 2026*

## ♪ Move with the music (Lab)
Right-click any slider (or its ♪) › **Move with the music…** — 0 tokens.
- **What drives it:** beat, bar, kick, snare, hi-hats, bass, mids, highs, vocals, drums or loudness.
- **How it moves:** pulse on each hit, follow the level, step through values, or random on each hit.
- Drag the two little handles under the slider (or around the knob) to set the range; a live meter shows the driver.
- Saved with the scene, templates and packs; **renders move exactly like the preview**. Works with live sound too.
- Also: "move with the beat" in Lab search or Ctrl/⌘+K.

## 🧹 Tidy memory
**Find it:** ⋯ AI setup › Memory › Tidy memory…
- A before/after list: facts said twice, old facts a newer one contradicts, wordy ones, outdated ones. Tick, Apply, one Undo.
- Lab-only facts can move to a **Lab** scope that only the Three Director reads.
- The AIs stop piling up memory: a fact they already know isn't added again; a changed fact replaces the old one.
- In a scripted two months of use, Claude's memory went from ≈ 280 to ≈ 120 tokens per message.

## 📂 Drag & drop (Mac and Windows)
- Drop files from Finder / Explorer on a chat (attached, pictures previewed) or on the Lab preview (a song becomes the song, a video a video layer, a picture a picture layer). A box says what will happen before you let go.
- Dropping a folder asks "Let this AI use this folder?" — never shared silently.
- ⌘V / Ctrl+V pastes screenshots and copied files into a chat or the Lab.
- Drag out of Hearth: a reply's pictures, code blocks (drag the "js · 12 lines" label), renders, templates, a Lab still (the preview's 📷).
- Big files are attached where they are; nothing is uploaded.

## 🤫 Calmer tips
- Notes that show by themselves now come **one at a time**, a few minutes apart, never while you type, drag or a reply runs, never over the chat box or the Lab preview.
- After an update: "Hearth updated" with Patch notes first, the rest one by one.
- **Settings › Hearth › Don't show tips** silences every optional tip. Errors, sign-in, offline and usage-limit notes still show.

## ⚡ A lighter touch
- Scrubbing the timeline, dragging Lab sliders and typing very long messages do far less work per move (e.g. scrubbing handlers ≈ 10× lighter).
- No more small hitch every two seconds while dragging in the Lab.
- A dragged Lab slider shows on the very next preview frame.

## Good to know
- A picture dropped on the Lab preview now adds a picture layer; **Alt+drop** opens "Match its look" as before.
- The ♪ button on a slider opens the new card; the older ♪ panel is under its **More ▾**.
- "Did you know?" left Settings for the single "Don't show tips" switch.
- A fact updated in "All agents" memory changes it for every AI.
