# Round 153: Trim Hearth — switch off what you don't use
*November 2026*

You asked to "remove features you don't use" so Hearth gets lighter. We can't see how you use Hearth from here, but
Hearth can: it has always counted, on your computer only, which buttons, commands and tools you use. So now there's a
panel that lists Hearth's extra features with how much **you** used them, and lets you switch off the ones you don't
need. Switched-off features **don't load at all**: no code, no background work, no menu entries.

## Where to find it

- **Settings › This computer & sync › Trim Hearth…**
- or **Ctrl+K** and type "trim"
- or the command bar (**Ctrl+;**) or any chat: `/trim`

## How it works

- Each row is one feature: its name, one line about what it does, **"used 12 times · last used 3 days ago"** (or
  **"never used on this computer"**), how heavy it is (KB of code, and whether it loads every time Hearth starts or only
  when you use it), and an **On / Off** switch.
- The least used are at the top.
- **Suggested** ticks off everything you haven't used for 30 days. It never switches anything by itself: look the list
  over, untick what you want to keep, then press **Apply**.
- **All on** puts everything back (then Apply).
- After Apply, Hearth may ask you to **Reload** (one click, your chats and work stay exactly as they are). Turning a
  feature back **on** works right away, no reload needed.

## What's safe

- **Your data stays.** Switching a feature off never deletes anything it saved (boards, captures, templates, settings…).
  Turn it back on and everything is where you left it.
- What Hearth is for can't be switched off: chats, projects, the Three.js Lab, Settings, sign-in, updates, sync and the
  safety nets (backups, versions, undo, recovery). They're listed at the bottom under "Always on".
- The setting is per computer: your Mac and your PC can be trimmed differently. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## What you can switch off (63 features)

Voice, phone remote & viewer, live links (OSC / MIDI / OBS / Ableton Link), "what did we decide?", hooks, desktop
notifications, Keep going & Auto-improve, Answered by Hearth, the cost preview by Send, the Quick idea window, Cost coach
& Usage left, the Usage dashboard, Studio pipelines, Credits, tours & tips, What's new & patch notes, button sparkle
effects, Capture & visual feedback, the mood board, the Creative board, app windows (Meshy, Suno, Figma…), Adobe apps,
publishing websites, Ready to post, the asset library, the Claude ⇄ Astra jam, ▶ Play (games), and about 35 Lab extras
(Variations, Mood dials, Lyrics, Perform, Scene versions, Recipes, Direction, Finish line, the gallery, packs…).

## How much lighter

With everything switched off, Hearth loads about **2 MB less code** when it starts (27 fewer feature groups, 86 fewer script files), wakes up less
often while you're not using it, and the menus (Ctrl+K, `/`, the keys list) only show what you kept.

A few leftovers nothing used any more were deleted: the Creative board's old game-preview page and two unused icons (one
from the removed Life tab).
