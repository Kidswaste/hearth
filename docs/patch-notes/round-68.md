# Round 68: tickets, leave reminders, a reference track and a broken-thing button
*October 2026*

## 🎟 My ticket
- **Keep your ticket with the show.** Open a show's card (Sidequest Studio › Map › ✦ Shows this week → a show) and
  click **🎟 Add my ticket**. Pick the PDF, screenshot or Wallet pass, or drag the file onto the card. Hearth keeps its
  own copy, and your file stays where it was.
- **At the door**, click **🎟 Open my ticket**. The ticket fills the whole window on white, ready to scan. Turn your
  screen brightness all the way up; **⛶ Full screen** makes it even bigger, and the screen won't go to sleep while it's
  open.
- On the day, the **✦ quest tracker** shows **🎟 Ticket ready · the show** at the top. Click it to open the ticket.
- The same button is on the **show quest** and on the night out in **🗓 Plan my weekend**.
- Already downloaded the ticket? When a file in **Downloads** is named like the show, the card asks **"Is this your
  ticket for …?"**. Click **Add it**, or **Not this one**. Hearth never moves or opens your Downloads files.
- After the show, the ticket stays with the night in **Shows I went to** (🎟 My ticket). Click **Remove (Trash)** in the
  ticket view when you don't need it any more. It goes to the Trash, not deleted for good.

## ⏰ Remind me to leave
- **New: ⏰ Remind me to leave.** Click it on a show (its card or its quest), on an errand's card, or on a slot of 🗓
  Plan my weekend. Hearth tells you 10 minutes before it's time to go: "Leave in 10 min for Arlo Parks — about 25
  min by Métro".
- **It knows the trip.** It counts from your 📍 I'm here pin, or from your 🏠 Home. Walks of up to 20 minutes are
  walking, and anything longer is a Métro estimate. It always says "about", and nothing is looked up online.
- **Set Home once**: the first time, the Paris map opens and you click where Home is. It stays on this computer. To
  move it: ✦ quest tracker › ⋯ More › Near you… › 🏠 Home.
- **Errands beat the closing time**: Hearth gets you to the shop 20 minutes before it closes.
- **The notification works with Hearth in the tray.** Click it to see the place on the map. **Snooze 10 min** or
  **Dismiss** without opening Hearth.
- **The ✦ quest tracker lists them** under "Leaving soon" ("Leave at 20:15"). Click × to turn one off.
- **If Hearth was closed when a reminder was due, it doesn't ring late.** You see "Missed" once instead.
- On the map, ⏰ pins show where you're going, and your 🏠 Home shows too. To hide them, switch off Layers › Leave
  reminders.

## 🎧 Reference
- **🎧 Reference** is a new button in the Sound desk's top row (Creative Playground › Serum 2 / Vital, next to
  Sessions). Drop a track you love on it (WAV, MP3, FLAC or M4A), or click it and choose a file.
- **A ⇄ B** is one big button that switches between your sound and the reference. Both play at the same loudness, and
  the panel says by how many dB the louder one was turned down.
- **Your sound** can be what the desk just played (its last 30 seconds), any sketch from Sessions, or a loop you
  trimmed from one. **↻ Measure again** takes a fresh 30 seconds of the desk.
- **Loudness in LUFS** for both, side by side: the whole reference, and the part that loops.
- **The loop starts on the reference's loudest 15 seconds.** Drag **Start** and **End** on its waveform to pick
  another part, or click **Whole track**.
- **Tone balance in plain words**: low, mid and high, yours against the reference, for example "Your low end is 4 dB
  lighter than the reference."
- **Where it plays**: through the window, on your interface when the window can pick it. The panel says which. The
  reference file is only read, and nothing is uploaded.

## 🪲 Something broke
- **New: 🪲 Something broke.** When Hearth acts up, click the **⋯** at the bottom of the left rail, then **Something
  broke** (right under "That was slow"). A small card says what went wrong lately in plain words, when it happened and
  which page you were on.
- On the card, **📋 Copy report for Claude** copies the details. Paste them into a Claude chat with a word about what
  you were doing, and the next update can fix exactly that. Nothing is sent anywhere by itself.
- The report never contains your chats, passwords or keys, or the names of your folders. Only Hearth's own folder
  shows.
- **↻ Restart Hearth** is right there on the card. **Reload this page only** reloads just the window and puts your
  screen, chats and Lab scene back. It waits while a reply is being written or a render is running.
- **If Hearth crashed or was force-closed**, the next start shows a small note, "Hearth closed unexpectedly last time".
  Click **🪲 See what happened** to see what went wrong before it closed. The last three are kept on this computer.
- It's also in the **Help** menu (on Windows, press Alt to show the menu bar).
- It costs nothing while nothing goes wrong: it only listens, and writes a small note when something breaks.

## ⚡ Lighter dialogs
- **Place details on the Paris map opens faster.** Clicking a pin's ⓘ Place details now takes about 12 ms of work
  instead of 15–21 ms on our test machine.
- **The same goes for every other Sidequest Studio dialog**, such as quest details, Events (the Outing desk) and
  proof review. While one is open, Hearth no longer redraws the parts of the window hidden behind it.
- **What you'll see:** the faint left rail and the Studio's top bar behind an open dialog now stay fully dark, like
  the page behind it already did. They are back the moment you close the dialog, and nothing moves or changes size.
- Round 66's map features ("I'm here", errands, Layers, park jams) were checked and were not the cause, so nothing
  was removed.
