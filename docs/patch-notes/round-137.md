# Round 137: see what the AI changed, and a tour of the new Lab
*November 2026*

## 🔍 What did the AI change?
- Under each Claude or Astra reply that changed a scene, a small folded line: **▸ Changed 3 layers · Review**. Open
  it to see each change in plain words ("Glow ring: amount 0.6 → 0.9", "New layer: Bass particles", "Orb: code: 2
  lines changed"), with before and after pictures of the scene.
- **↶ Undo this layer** takes back just that layer and keeps the rest of the reply (one Undo). **Show the code
  change** shows the lines added and removed. **Keep all** closes it; **Undo all** takes the whole reply back.
- When a reply only moved sliders, **Keep some…** lets you tick the moves to keep.

## ◈ A tour of what's new in the Lab
- The first time you open the Lab after this update, a small note offers a 2-minute tour (once only; **Later** or ✕
  and it won't come back by itself). Twelve short steps ring the real buttons, with **Show me**; it never sends
  anything to an AI and never changes your scene (steps that would change something use a copy, removed after).
- **Lab features…** (Ctrl/⌘+K, or ◈ New in the Lab on the "Since you were away" card): every new Lab feature since
  late October, one line each, with a search and **Open**.
- Fixed: a stray "**" on the "Since you were away" card.
