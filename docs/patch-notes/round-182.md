# Round 182: a clean Projects screen
*November 2026*

## ✨ What a project is going to be
- **A project's card is now its cover, its name and one line**: what it is going to be. Write the line from the card
  ("What is it going to be?" appears when you point at a card without one) or from the ⋯ menu. The counts and the
  three little buttons are gone from the cards; they are in the tooltip and the ⋯ menu.
- **Continue**: the project you last worked on sits on top as one wide card, with its chat, scene and render one
  click away.

## 🎮 🎵 🎞 Games, music and videos
- **＋ New project starts with three choices: Game, Music or Video.** Each shows only its own ways to start (a game
  never offers the audio visualizer, music never offers the game start).
- **Projects has three tabs with counts.** A project you made before is sorted by how it started; change it any time
  from ⋯ › Kind.
- "What is it going to be?" on the new-project card becomes the line on the project's card.

## 🗂 Older version
- **Projects made for the old player are folded under "Older version"** at the bottom of the screen, closed until you
  open it. They are not changed: they open, edit and render as before.
- A project counts as older when none of its scenes is in the new format. Wrong for one of them? ⋯ › Move back with
  the current projects (or ⋯ › Move to "Older version" for the opposite).

## 💡 What could come next
- Under your projects, and on each project's page: **follow-ups of the project** (a sequel, a trailer, its theme as a
  visual…) and **ideas for a different series**, one of each kind. One click opens the new-project card filled in.
- **✦ Ideas from the AI** asks once for ideas that fit that project and what else you made (one small AI call, only
  when you click).
- A follow-up keeps the **series** of the project it comes from. The series shows under the card, and you can list
  one series alone.

## 🧰 Small conveniences
- ★ **Pin** a project to the top (⋯ menu, or P on a card).
- **Order**: last worked on, newest first, or by name.
- **Keyboard**: `/` jumps to the search, `N` starts a new project; on a card Enter opens, F2 renames, the arrows move.
- The big animated banner only shows when you switch it on (Projects › ⋯ › Animated frontpage).

## Good to know
- I have not seen this with your real projects. Clink and Ascend to Heaven will be under "Older version" if all
  their scenes are in the old format: move them back from the ⋯ menu if you want them on top.
- Your existing projects have no line yet unless they had a description.
