# Round 129: from plan to first draft
*November 2026*

## ✦ Build a first draft
- The plan card at the top of a project's Lab chat now has **✦ Build a first draft** (all the steps, or up to the step
  you pick). One click and the director builds them in one go: layers in the brief's palette, keyed to the song's
  sections where the plan says so, each step ticked as it lands, then a check on screen.
- Before you click, the card shows roughly what it will cost. While it runs: "Building step 2 of 5…" and **Stop**
  (what's done stays). **↶ Undo the draft** takes the whole draft back in one step. Your own layers are left alone.
- Works with Claude or Astra.

## ◆ The project's look on the scene
- When the scene belongs to a project with a palette, a small **◆ strip** of its colours shows in the Lab toolbar.
  **Apply** recolours your layers' colour sliders to it (dark stays dark, light stays light): point at Apply to
  preview, click to keep, one Undo (or Ctrl/⌘+Z) to go back. Your locked sliders are kept.
- **⋯**: all layers or the selected one, Subtle or Full, **🖼 Take the palette from the moodboard** (read on your
  computer, no AI), **💾 Save as a look** to switch back and forth.
- The AI can apply the project's look the same way.

## ➜ Next step, and project templates
- Under each director reply in a project, a small **Next: …** chip suggests the next step (from the brief, the AI's
  own suggestions or the scene). It's sent only when you click it; ✕ or typing hides it. Turn it off in ⋯ AI setup ›
  Habits.
- Project menu › **Save as a template…** keeps a project's main scene, palette, size, frame rate and brief (and the
  song or moodboard if you tick them), with a cover. New projects can **Start from** it under "My templates", with
  the plan card already there.
