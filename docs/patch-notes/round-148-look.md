# Round 148: the Lab looks like your moodboard
*November 2026*

## 🎛 Instrument panels
- The Lab's panels now read like the audio-visualiser HUD on your moodboard: deep navy-black, electric blue, thin
  hairline frames with little corner ticks, a faint dot grid behind the panels and soft scanlines.
- Each panel has a tiny numbered header with live readings on the right:
  - **01 PREVIEW** (just above the picture, never on it): the frame rate with a little bar graph of the last readings,
    and the preview quality (for example "auto · 50 %").
  - **02 TIMELINE** (on the timeline's top line): the tempo, the meter, a 4-square beat meter, the bar you're on and
    the time. It follows when you click or drag to a new spot.
  - **03 LAYERS & KNOBS**: the layer count next to the name.
- Numbers are in a mono font with even widths, with a slight glow on the important ones.
- The Forgeheart ember is still there as the one "hot" colour: the playhead, the selected layer, an open fold
  (♩ Beat ▾, ⚡ Sync ▾…) and the heaviest layer's cost badge. Sliders and favourites in the Lab turn blue.
- Small moving touches: a live dot that gently pulses on each header, a slow light sweep along the preview's header,
  the glint on the timeline and the Layers & knobs head. Everything stops the moment the scene plays (smooth playback
  first), and none of it moves if you chose calmer motion (Appearance › Motion: calm / off, or your computer's
  "reduce motion").

## 📄 A light "Plate" look
- A new light variant inspired by your lavender technical plates: pale panels, indigo accents, a fine grid, headers
  labelled **PL.01 PREVIEW**, **PL.02 TIMELINE**, **PL.03 LAYERS & KNOBS**. The picture and the song's lanes stay dark.

## Where to switch
- **Lab ⋯** (top right of the Lab) › **View & help** › **Lab look**. Each click goes to the next one:
  **Forgeheart → Plate → Plain → Forgeheart**. **Plain** is the flat look without headers or motion.

Nothing moved: every button, menu and key is where it was.
