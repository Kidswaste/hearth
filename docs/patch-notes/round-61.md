## ✦ A quest tracker everywhere
- One small ✦ chip in the rail on every screen shows your quest and its next step. Click it (or Ctrl/⌘+Shift+O) to check off a step, follow another quest or take today's picks. `/quest next`, `/quest done`, `/quest track …`. Nothing in the ▶ Play window, no background work.

## 🎹 Live Serum 2 / Vital
- Play and tweak live through your audio interface on **ASIO** (the UA interface first in the list), with WASAPI as the fallback. Buffer 64–1024, latency / load / dropouts shown. Each instrument stays loaded, so switching is instant; the sound keeps playing while presets load or the plugin window is open. Your MIDI controller works directly.
