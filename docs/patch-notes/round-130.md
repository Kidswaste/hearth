# Round 130: takes, the song map and a critique
*November 2026*

## ✦ 3 takes
- Beside Send in a director's chat (or any chat linked to a Lab scene), **✦ 3 takes** turns your ask into three
  versions: A all the way, B colour & light, C motion & rhythm. A card first shows the ask, the project's palette and
  roughly what it costs; nothing runs until you click **Make 3 takes**.
- The three play side by side, live and with the song, each with one line on what it changed. Click the one you
  like: it's applied as one Undo. ✕, Esc or a new ask throws them away, and your scene is never touched before you
  pick. **■ Stop** works any time. When one take changed only colours and another only motion, **Mix** combines them.
- Also `/takes <your ask>`.

## ✦ Map to the song
- In the timeline's song row (once the song is analysed), **✦ Map** proposes, with no AI: a cue for each section of
  the song on its downbeat (with a saved look that fits, if you have one), a jump on each drop on the main layer's
  intensity or glow, and kick / snare links on fitting sliders.
- It all shows as dashed "ghosts" on the timeline and in a list by section. **Keep all**, **Keep this section**, or ✓
  / ✕ each item; every keep is one Undo. **✦ Ask the AI to refine** shows its cost first.
- Also in Sections ▾ and `/map-song`. The AI can use it too.

## ✦ Critique
- **✦ Critique** in the director dock (or `/critique` in a director's chat) looks at 3–4 frames of the scene at the
  song's key moments, its layers and sliders, your brief and the scene's health, and gives **3 concrete
  improvements**, each with one line on why. The cost shows first; nothing is sent or captured until you click.
- **Apply** on a slider or keyframe change lands at once (one Undo); anything bigger goes to the director as your
  message. ✕ hides a suggestion. It remembers its last 5 critiques per project so it doesn't repeat itself, and
  checks the brief's risks off.

## ◆ Tidier toolbar
- The project's palette no longer sits open in the Lab toolbar: it's one small **◆** button now. Click it to show
  the colours, **Apply** and **⋯** in a small panel under it; Esc or a click elsewhere folds it away.
