# Round 141: how close am I, and say it in plain words
*November 2026*

## ◎ How close am I?
- In 🖼 References, a picture's or clip's card › ⋯ › **◎ Match against this** (or a moodboard picture's right-click,
  or Ctrl/⌘+K "How close am I?"). A small **◎ N %** chip above the stats line shows how close your frame is to it,
  updated twice a second while you tweak, worked out on your computer at almost no cost.
- Click it for the parts in plain words: **Colours**, **Light**, **Energy** (detail), and **Motion** for a clip
  ("yours is darker").
- **◎ Closer** nudges your colour and light sliders (and the Grade layer, if there is one) toward the reference, keeping
  only what raises the score: one Undo. **✦ Ask the AI for the rest** shows its cost first and sends no picture.

## ⌁ Say it in plain words
- In the Lab, press **Ctrl/⌘+;** and type: "glow 0.8", "more glow", "glow 0.8 on the drop", "add bloom", "hide
  sparks", "solo ring", "energy up", "follow bass", "jump to drop", "render reel". A line shows what will happen
  before you press Enter; each one is one Undo. ↑ / ↓ for history, ? for what it understands.
- Read on your computer, no AI. When it doesn't understand, it offers **✦ Ask the director** (sent only if you click).
