# Round 67: direction, honest credits, errands, invites and loops
*October 2026*

## ✦ Steer a scene without describing it
- **New: the ✦ Direction chip** in the three.js Lab, next to the scene picker. It opens one card with everything below.
- **✦ Try 4 ways**: type the idea in a few words (or nothing) and click **Try 4 ways**. Four helpers each make one rough
  take on their own copy, each really different: colour and light · motion · form · texture. The crew panel shows the
  cost first; click **Start 4 helpers**, then pick the one you like.
- **This one, with that one's light**: once there are takes, the card shows **Keep #1 with its light from #3** (light,
  colours, motion, shapes, camera or texture). Click **Ask the director** and it brings in only that part.
- **◯ Mark it up**: click it and the picture freezes. Drag to circle a part, then choose **More of this** (green) or
  **Wrong** (red), with a few words if you like. **Send to the director** sends the marked picture. Cancel sends nothing.
- **Plain sliders**: **Calmer ↔ Busier**, **Colder ↔ Warmer**, **Softer ↔ Harsher**. Drag to preview, let go to keep it
  (one Ctrl+Z undoes it). **Back to the middle** puts the scene back. They move the scene's named sliders; a scene
  without any says so.
- **Vision card**: **Add this frame** (up to 3 pictures of how it should look), a few words, and one "It should feel
  like…" sentence. Each new version is measured against it, and the ✦ chip turns orange ("drifting a little",
  "drifting away") before it piles up. **Send the vision to the director** shares it.
- **⑂ Branches, not resets**: in the ⏱ time machine, go back to any older version and keep working. That now starts a
  branch inside the same scene; nothing is thrown away. The strip shows ⑂ where a branch starts, and the Direction card
  lists each branch with **Go there**.

## 🖼 Moodboard: inspired by, not copied
- **Dragging a tile into a chat now sends what it feels like, not the picture**: its colours (with how much of each),
  brightness, contrast, warmth, texture and grain, busy or clean, composition, shapes, and for clips the motion and
  cuts. Want the actual picture? Hover the tile and click **Picture**, or right-click › Send the actual picture.
- **What I like about this**: a line under each tile. Click it and write why you pinned it. It goes first, as the
  strongest signal.
- **Close to it … Only its spirit**: a slider under the colour strip. "Only its spirit" sends just the mood and says
  plainly not to copy the subject or layout. The board remembers where you left it.
- **Mix 2–3 tiles**: click two or three tiles, check the **Mix** card, then **Send the mix**.

## 🤝 Astra and Swarm work again
- **Collaborations can see the scene.** Side by side, debates, councils and quick asks started from a scene's chat
  give every AI a fresh picture of that scene and its layer list. The card says **👁 saw the scene**.
- **Council reads its own reviews.** With two rounds, the members review each other and the final answer now uses
  those reviews (they used to be dropped).
- **Second opinions** from a scene's chat look at that chat's scene, even when another one is on screen.
- **If Astra can't use the Lab tools, it says so** under its reply, in orange, with **Run this director on Claude**.
- **Give Astra the Lab with a click**: an agent's settings now have **Can see and change the Lab scene**.
- **🐝 Swarm starts in the Lab** without needing a second AI set up.
- **Less lag**: Astra's helpers from councils, jams and crews close as soon as their turn ends.

## 🎬 After Effects beside Hearth
- **Hearth and After Effects now work at the same time.** A script for After Effects (from an AI, or **Run in After
  Effects** under code in a chat) runs as a job: a progress chip shows while AE works, and a note says when it's done
  or why it failed. You and the AI keep working in the Lab meanwhile.
- **No more stuck error box in AE**: a failing script now reports back to Hearth in words, instead of leaving a dialog
  in After Effects that blocks it until you click it.

## 🏷 Honest credits
- **Credits** (Lab toolbar, before 📷) shows who did what on the scene on screen: the visual code (AI-drafted,
  hand-tuned by you: how many edits and slider moves), the sound (what you played and tuned in the Sound desk) and the
  direction. **Copy** puts it in one paragraph to paste under a post. Anything Hearth didn't record says "not recorded".
- **How it was made**: pick **9:16** or **16:9** and a folder, then **Make the video**: a short reel of the scene's
  versions with plain title cards. It's saved on this computer; nothing is uploaded.

## ⚑ Finish lines
- **⚑ Finish line** (next to the scene picker): give the piece a size (**10 s**, **30 s** or **Full**) and a "Done
  when" list to tick. Click a line to change its words. When every line is ticked, the card says it's done and offers
  **Record it**.
- **✦ Polish this** turns what's left into exactly three small, concrete steps. **Track it** adds them as a side quest;
  **Track + guide me** also opens the step-by-step guide. Made here, no AI tokens.


## 🐢 That was slow
- **New: 🐢 That was slow.** When Hearth stutters or freezes, click the **⋯** at the bottom of the left rail, then
  **That was slow**. A small card says what the last hiccup was in plain words, for example "The last hiccup: 1.4 s,
  while you were in the Lab (main process busy with your settings)".
- After a freeze of a second or more, the **⋯ turns amber** for two minutes so you know where to click.
- On the card, **📋 Copy report for Claude** copies the details. Paste them into a Claude chat so the next update can
  fix exactly that. Nothing is sent anywhere by itself.
- The card also counts **hiccups over 500 ms today, next to yesterday's**, with a small bar for each of the last 7
  days. It's an easy way to see whether an update made Hearth smoother.
- It's also in the **Help** menu (on Windows, press Alt to show the menu bar).
- It costs nothing while you don't click it: it only reads what Hearth's smoothness watch already notes, and it never
  starts a recording that could freeze Hearth itself.

## 🛍 Errands
- **Errands with opening hours.** For things like "get AirPods at Apple Opéra": click **＋ Errand** in the ✦ quest
  tracker or at the end of the Paris map's chip row, type what you need, and pick a shop.
- **Shops are suggested as you type.** "AirPods" puts Apple Opéra first. The list holds 13 well-known Paris shops
  (Apple, Fnac, BHV, Galeries Lafayette, Printemps, Gibert, Monoprix, two 24 h pharmacies) with their usual hours.
  Always check before going: Hearth doesn't look anything up online.
- **Anywhere else**: click **📍 Click a spot on the map…** in the card, click the map, name the spot, and type its
  hours (for example "Mon–Sat 10:00–20:00 · Sun closed").
- **Every errand has a badge**: Open now · closes at 20:00, Opens at 10:00, Closed today… It's on the map pin, in the
  tracker and in "Close to you" when your 📍 pin is near. It handles Sundays, lunch breaks and late nights.
- **Done ✓** next to an errand completes it as a quest (+15 XP). No proof is needed.
- **The map's Layers panel has an "Errands" switch**, on by default.
- **Fix:** the switches in the map's Layers panel now respond to a real mouse click. Before, clicking one moved the
  map instead.

## 👋 Bring someone
- **Bring someone to a show**: open a show's card (Sidequest Studio › Map › ✦ Shows this week → a show, or click a
  venue on the map) and click **👋 Bring someone**. It's also on the night out in **🗓 Plan my weekend**.
- Pick a tone (**Casual**, **Hyped** or **Low-pressure**) and **Français** or **English**. Hearth writes a short invite
  with the show, the date, the venue and the ticket link. It always gives your friend an easy out ("no worries if
  not"). You can edit the text.
- Click **Copy**, or **Open in WhatsApp / Messages / Instagram**. These only open the app with your text ready.
  Hearth never sends anything: you choose who and press send.
- Want it worded differently? **✨ Rewrite with Claude** asks Claude once, only when you click it.
- After sending, click **✓ I sent it** to log "Invited someone" on a matching social quest, like "Before the show".
  The card shows which quest first. Only your own invite is saved, never who it went to or their answer.

## 🎛 Sketches on pads
- **Your sketches are playable now.** Go to the Sound desk and click **Sessions**. Every 30-second sketch has two new
  buttons.
- **🎛 Put on a pad**: pick an empty pad in your Live set (the next free one shows as **＋**). The sketch goes there, ready
  for your open mic or park jam. Click **🎤 Open the Live set** to play it.
- **✂ Trim loop**: a small waveform with **Start** and **End** handles. They snap to bars at your session's tempo, or a
  tempo Hearth hears in the sketch. Type your own BPM if it's off.
  - **▶ Preview loop** plays the loop over and over while you adjust it.
  - **💾 Save loop** saves it as a new WAV next to the sketch. Your original sketch is never changed.
- Saved loops show under their sketch, with ▶, 🎛 Put on a pad and Remove loop (to the Trash). They also show up in
  **Arrange loops** and in the Live set's "Park takes" list.

## 🧹 Fixes
- **Visual feedback no longer risks wiping your review history.** If the history file can't be read for a moment, your
  new review is still shown, the old ones are left alone, and a short note tells you.
- **On Windows, Hearth now finds PowerShell by its full path.** This makes the check for OneDrive and iCloud files that
  are still downloading more dependable.
- **On the Mac, sync handles file names that differ only by capital letters or accents** as the same file, as Windows
  already did.
- **Eleven more places show errors in plain words** instead of the computer's raw message: the offline check, the quest
  helper, the Outing desk's visit time, Sidequest's proof and evidence pickers, and Visual feedback's cost line.
- **The Sidequest map speed test works again.** It now opens the Outing desk from Map › Events.
- **The new map layers from round 66 (Park jams, Paris place quests) don't slow the map down.** The test measured no
  difference.
- **Opening a place's details on the map is a little slower than in round 65** (about 21–23 ms of work against a 16 ms
  target). It's flagged for a closer look.
