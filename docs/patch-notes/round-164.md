# Round 164: fewer pieces to load
*November 2026*

## What got lighter

- **Hearth loads its features in fewer, larger pieces.** Small parts that always arrive together (chat commands, the
  Kit and node view, the director's dock, safety notes, Lite mode's menus…) now come in as one: 185 pieces became 171,
  and the start reads 14 small marker files instead of 51. Nothing looks or works differently, and every feature can
  still be switched off in **Settings › Trim Hearth**.
