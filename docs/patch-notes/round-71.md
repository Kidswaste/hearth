# Round 71: tours, a quick idea button, studio hours, food before shows, a faster Sidequest
*October 2026*

## ✦ Tours of what's new
- **✦ Since you were last here.** The first time you open Hearth after updating, one small card says how many updates
  you missed and offers the **1-minute tours**. Click **Take the tours**, or pick one, or **Later**. Later means it
  won't come back by itself.
- **Five short tours**: 🌙 Paris nights · 🗺 Sidequest · 🎛 Music · 🪲 Help when it breaks · ✦ In the Lab. Each step
  is one sentence. Click **Show me** and Hearth takes you there and puts a ring on the actual button: Plan my
  weekend, Bring someone, My ticket, Remind me to leave, Before I go, Eat nearby, Follow, I'm here, Layers, Errands,
  your photos, Sessions, sketches on pads, Reference, Map my controller, Send to my DAW, That was slow, Something
  broke, Direction, Finish line, Credits and the moodboard.
- Just looking changes nothing. Nothing happens until you click the button itself.
- Any time: click the ✦ chip in the left rail, then **✦ New tours**. They are also in What's new, and in the
  patch notes of rounds 66–70 (✦ Show me).

## 💡 Quick idea
- **💡 Quick idea, from anywhere.** Click the Hearth icon in your tray / menu bar › **💡 Quick idea…**, or press
  **Ctrl+Alt+I** in any app. A small window opens on top. Hearth's main window stays where it is.
- **Type it, or hold 🎙 and say it.** The mic is only on while you hold the button. The voice memo stays on this
  computer.
- **Click where it goes:** **🎵 Music** (Sound desk › Sessions), **✦ Quest** (Sidequest › Find a quest › Saved ideas),
  **📁 Project** (pick a recent one: the idea goes in its notes) or **💬 Later**.
- **Enter saves to the same place as last time.** Esc closes the window and keeps your words.
- **Everything you saved is in 💡 Ideas**: the rail's **⋯** menu, or the ✦ quest tracker (a "💡 N ideas waiting" row,
  and its ⋯ More). Each idea has **Move to…** and **Delete**. Deleted ideas wait in a Trash with **Restore** for
  30 days.
- Music ideas also show at the bottom of the Sound desk's **Sessions** panel, with ▶ to play the memo.
- To change the key, or turn it off: **Settings › Advanced › Keys › 💡 Quick idea from anywhere**. If another app
  already uses the key, Hearth leaves it off and tells you.

## 🎛 Studio hours
- **🎛 Studio hours.** Click the ✦ quest chip in the rail. Under "This week" there's a new row, "This week: 3 h 20 making
  music", with a little Monday-to-Sunday bar. It counts your Sound desk sessions (Serum 2 / Vital), logged or not.
- **Click that row** for the Studio hours card: your days this week, your last 8 weeks, your longest session, Serum 2 vs
  Vital, and how many sketches, trimmed loops and park sessions you had.
- **An optional weekly goal.** In the card, click "Off · Set one" and pick 2, 3, 4 or 6 hours. It shows as a soft ring in
  the tracker. If a week falls short, nothing is said about it: the card just says "New week, fresh start".
- **Sunday evening**, if you made music that week, a single quiet note: "You made music 3 times this week 🎛". Never on
  a week without music.
- "Sessions in the Sound desk ›" at the bottom of the card takes you straight to your sessions and sketches.
- Fixed: opening the Sound desk from another place right after starting Hearth no longer stops on the Playground's
  home page.

## 🍜 Eat nearby
- **New: 🍜 Eat nearby.** Open a show (Paris map › Shows this week › the show) and click **🍜 Eat nearby**. You get food
  stops within about 10 minutes' walk of the venue that are open before doors, closest first. Your ★ starred and ✓
  visited places come first.
- **Also in 🗓 Plan my weekend** (on the show night) and at the bottom of **🎒 Before I go**.
- **Go here first** puts the stop on your ⏰ leave reminder. Hearth works out the trip to the food stop, gives you 45
  minutes to eat, adds the walk to the venue, and tells you when to leave. Change **Eat time** at the top of the list
  if you need longer or shorter.
- **Show on map** takes you to the place on the Paris map.
- **"Hours unknown"** means Hearth doesn't know when it's open. Click **Add hours…** on the row and type them once (for
  example "Tue–Sat 19:00–23:00"). They're kept on this computer, and places closed before doors then drop out of the
  list.
- The reminder's notification and the ✦ tracker say where you're eating first. Click **✓ First stop** to go straight
  to the venue again.

## ⚡ Faster Sidequest
- **Switching between Today, Map and Progress in Life is snappier with a big journal.** Before, each switch made Hearth
  lay out the whole new page in one go, every card included, even when you were already at the top. Now it only lays
  out what's on screen. On a full journal, a switch went from about 0.12–0.17 s of work to 0.03–0.06 s.
- **Opening Life and going straight to the Map is quicker.** From a chat to the map's pins took about 0.7–0.8 s on the
  test machine and now takes 0.4–0.55 s.
- **Closed windows in Life no longer pile up out of sight.** Closing the Events desk (or any Life window) while Hearth
  wasn't redrawing, for example while minimized, could leave invisible copies behind. They're now always cleared away.
