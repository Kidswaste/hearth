# Round 145: perfect loops with sound
*November 2026*

## ∞ Perfect-loop MP4s now carry the song
- An **MP4 · perfect loop** comes out with the song's sound under it: exactly the loop's stretch of the song, as long
  as the video to the sample.
- The seam is blended over a few milliseconds (the moment just after the loop fades into its start), so when the file
  repeats on Instagram, a website or a wallpaper, the sound goes round without a click.
- Where: the Render card (⇪ Export… › Format **More ▾ › MP4 · perfect loop**, or the **Seamless loop** preset), and
  right-click the picture › **⇪ Make & export › ∞ Make it loop › ⇪ Render the loop**.
- A scene with no song stays silent, as before. GIFs stay silent (GIFs have no sound).

## ♪ Sound on / off
- With MP4 · perfect loop picked, the Render card's Quality row shows **♪ Sound on**. Click it for **♪ Sound off**: a
  silent loop. Hearth remembers your choice for the next loops (from the card and from ∞ Make it loop).

## Also
- ∞ Make it loop's render is now one file made in one go (it used to make a silent video, then a second
  "+ sound" copy).
