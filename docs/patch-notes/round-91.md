# Round 91: projects end to end, a full day stays fast, the last Lab hitches
*October 2026*

## 📁 Projects, checked end to end on both computers
- Ctrl/⌘+K now finds your projects: type a project's name and press Enter to switch to it.
- Renaming a project updates its name at the top straight away.
- Drop files from Explorer or Finder on a project's Desk to add them to the project, or drag a chat there from the
  chats list.
- A scene you make in the Lab while a project is open (New, Duplicate, a template) now belongs to that project.
- A project's cover now shows on your other computer too.
- Putting the same chat in a project on both computers no longer brings back something you took out on the other.
- Renamed a project on both computers at once? You keep one project: "Keep both" in the sync panel names it with both
  names instead of making a second project, and the computer whose name was replaced tells you, with a button to
  use your name again.

## ⏳ Fast after a full day
- **Hearth no longer collects hidden copies of the director's bar.** Every time you opened a project or the Lab, the
  little activity bar above the docked chat was rebuilt, and the old one stayed in memory with all its buttons until
  you quit. Over a day of switching projects that kept adding up. Old bars now really go away.
- **We tested a whole working day in fast-forward.** Projects, the giant chat, sending messages, the Lab's scenes,
  Settings, the Usage window, the sync panel, search and syncing with your other computer, over and over. Memory,
  background timers and how busy Hearth is while you're away all stay flat over the day now. When you leave it alone,
  Hearth uses next to no processor time, and that doesn't creep up the longer it stays open.

## 🎬 The last Lab hitches
- **Lights switching on and off no longer stutter.** When a scene turns a light off, removes it, or turns one on, the
  Lab gets ready for the change between two frames and then shows it, instead of freezing for a moment.
- **Shadows are ready before they appear.** The first time a light starts casting shadows, or a new kind of object
  starts casting them, the Lab prepares the shadows in the background first.
- **Switching to another scene inside the same sketch is smooth.** Going from a menu to a level, for example, now keeps
  the last picture on screen for a moment while the new scene gets ready, instead of sticking.
- **Saving a big picture again is smooth.** When you save a 4K picture that a scene uses again (from Photoshop, for
  example), the new version is now sent to the graphics card a strip at a time, while the old one keeps showing. Then
  it swaps in, with no jolt.
- **Big pictures arrive at the speed your graphics card can handle.** On a fast card they come in a few bigger pieces
  and show sooner. On a slow one they come in more, smaller pieces so nothing stutters.
