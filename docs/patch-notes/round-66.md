# Round 66: plan, pin and keep your sessions
*October 2026*

## 🗓 Plan my weekend
- **Plan my weekend**: from Thursday evening to Sunday, click the ✦ quest chip in the left rail, then **🗓 Plan my
  weekend** (it's also in the tracker's ⋯ More menu). Hearth suggests a balanced weekend:
  - a show you could go to on Friday night
  - music at the park on the better afternoon
  - a Paris place you haven't been to, close to the park
  - a studio session on your open music project
- Each line has **Swap** (show me the next best option) and **Keep** (click to leave it out). Click **Accept plan** and
  the kept ones become quests on their days, each with a time. The ✦ chip then shows each day's quest. Click
  **See my week** to move one to another day.
- It works with no internet. It uses the shows Hearth already saved and tells you so. Without a weather forecast, it
  reminds you to check the sky before the park. Nothing is uploaded, and it uses no AI tokens.

## 📍 I'm here
- **New: "📍 I'm here" on the Paris map.** In Sidequest Studio, open **Map** and click **📍 I'm here** (top right of
  the map), then click where you are. No GPS, nothing sent: the spot stays on this computer and fades after 3 hours.
- **Close to you** opens right on the map. It shows the nearest place quests, tonight's shows and park-jam spots, with
  "about N min walk" on each. Click **Start this quest** to begin one, or **Show on map** to see where it is.
- **In the ✦ quest tracker**, a **📍 Near me** row shows while your pin is set. Click it to jump back to what's close.
  You can also set the pin from the tracker's **⋯ More › Near you… › 📍 I'm here**.
- **Move my pin** and **Clear pin** are at the bottom of the Close to you panel. Click **×** to hide the list while
  keeping the pin.

## 🎛 Music sessions
- **Nice session — your music sessions are kept now.** After 10 minutes in the Sound desk (Creative Playground → Serum 2
  / Vital), a small strip shows up under the desk's buttons: "Nice session — 23 min."
- **💾 Save a 30-second sketch** keeps the last 30 seconds of what you just played, as a WAV, in your project's
  sketches folder. Nothing is recorded to disk until you click, and nothing is uploaded.
- **✓ Log it** puts the session in your **Sessions** list and in **My Paris** (the Paris map's timeline). It's also
  offered as proof to the music quests it fits: the park sketch, Finish a track, your open-mic set. The review and XP
  stay your choice.
- **✕** hides the strip for the rest of that session.
- **Sessions** (a new button in the Sound desk's top row) lists your past sessions with ▶ play buttons for their
  sketches. Remove sketch sends a file to the Trash.
- The clock only counts time you're actually making music: a desk left open on its own stops counting after 3 minutes.
  Playing your MIDI controller counts.
- Caveat: Arrange's ▶ Play isn't in the 30-second sketch (it plays outside the instrument). Everything played through
  Serum 2 / Vital and the Live set is.

## 🗺 Map fixes
- **The map's + and − buttons are always clickable now.** Before, when you scrolled Sidequest Studio's map page, the
  tab bar at the top (Today · Map · Progress) slid over them. Now they move down to just below whatever bar is in the
  way.
- **New "Layers" button on the map** (bottom left, first in the row of chips). Click it to see every layer by name,
  with a switch for each: Food & stops, Nights & shows, Park jams, Paris place quests, My walks, Visited · My Paris.
  Close it with Done.
- **Park jam spots are back on the map**, the parks picked for an outdoor music session.
- **Every Paris place quest is back.** Zoom in twice with + and the guide's places appear, each with its quest. Click
  one, then **Start this quest**. The zoomed-out view stays calm.
- **New layers start switched on.** Anything you switched off yourself stays off, and the Layers button shows how many
  are off.

## 🚀 Starting Hearth
- Settings › Starting and closing › Open on start works again: if you picked a screen there (the Lab, a chat…),
  Hearth opens on it. With no choice, Hearth opens on Projects, as before.
- That menu's first choice now says "Projects", which is what it does.
- Starting Hearth does a little less work when it opens somewhere other than Projects: the projects part loads in
  a quiet moment instead of right after the first screen.
