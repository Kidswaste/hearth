# Round 39: Recipes, Smart learns from you, a lighter Lab, calmer Settings, power-wise
*October 2026*

## 📜 Recipes (Lab)
Do your usual finishing steps once, replay them on any scene in one click — 0 tokens.
**Find it:** right-click the preview › ✦ More › 📜 Recipes › ● Record a recipe (also the toolbar's ⋯ and Ctrl/⌘+K).
- A red chip counts the steps while it records: effects, looks, palettes, sliders, ♪ music links, frame size, All formats, polish, look match, gallery layers, export settings.
- **Use a recipe…** shows which steps fit this scene; ones that don't are skipped with a note. The whole replay is **one Undo**.
- Edit, run on several scenes, share as a pack, or ask the director: "use my Finish recipe on this scene".

## 🧠 Smart learns from you
- When you keep asking for more on one kind of message (code fixes, Lab builds…), Smart starts picking higher for it; when you keep choosing lighter, it goes lighter. All local, no tokens.
- It's careful: several signs first, one step at most, and it takes back a lighter pick quickly if it falls short.
- See it in ⋯ AI setup › How hard it thinks ("Smart has learned: code fixes → Deepest"); ✕ forgets.

## 🪶 The Lab stays light for hours
- Switching scenes, re-running and director edits no longer keep the old scene's data (40 switches: 54 MB before, 6 MB now).
- Sounds, videos and cameras a scene started stop when you leave it.
- If the preview ever gets heavy, it tidies itself; if it can't, one tip offers ⟲ Restart.

## ⚙ Calmer Settings
- Four plain sections — Look & size · Your AIs · This computer & sync · Updates & what's new — plus a folded Advanced.
- 27 controls at first sight → 17; nothing removed. Every fold says where it stands in one line.
- Advanced › Backup and files › **Backups…** opens your backups (it used to need a command).

## 🔋 Power-wise (Mac and Windows)
- Keeps your computer awake only while it renders, exports, records, presents, downloads something big, updates, or an AI works on a long answer — then lets it sleep. A small ☕ in the rail shows while it does; click it to let it sleep anyway.
- In the background Hearth is far quieter (about 1 wake-up a minute instead of ~48).
- On battery (or Low Power Mode / battery saver): calmer animations, slower checks, lighter Lab. Plugged in, all back.

## 🐞 Fixed
- The Before / after key no longer throws an error when a key reaches the window itself.
- Searching Settings opens nested folds and shows the matching row straight away.
- "cheaper" in Find anything finds Spending again.

## Good to know
- Size of everything in Settings is now a drop-down list (the Appearance window keeps its buttons).
- Closing the lid still sleeps the computer, even while ☕ shows.
- Smart's learning reads your own choices; a "try again" meant as "another version" counts as "wanted more".
