# Round 101: new Lab layers appear without a stutter
*November 2026*

## 🎬 New layers appear smoothly on every graphics card
- **Adding a layer no longer makes the Lab preview stick, on computers that can't prepare shaders in the
  background** (many Macs, for example). A new layer, a 3D model or an effect-heavy scene now gets ready between frames
  while the rest of the picture keeps moving, then appears a moment later. Opening the Lab is as fast as before.

## 💾 Chat saves, measured
- No change you'll notice. We checked whether saving long chats could be made faster. Saving a message in a long chat
  already takes less than a hundredth of a second, and the faster way made no difference you could measure, so we kept
  the way that works.
