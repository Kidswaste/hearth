# Round 107: a calmer Lab on fast screens, a quicker start
*November 2026*

## 🎬 The Lab rests between frames on a 240 Hz screen
- **The Lab rests between its frames on a fast screen.** On a 240 Hz screen the Lab already drew at most 60 pictures a
  second (30 when you're in another app), but it still woke up 240 times a second to decide not to draw. It now sleeps
  until just before the next picture it will draw: about half as many wake-ups while you watch a scene, and about a
  fifth as many while you're in Spotify or your DAW with the Lab open behind it. The picture looks exactly the same.
- **A model you used earlier no longer keeps the Lab busy in the background.** While the Lab is hidden, a model waiting
  in its memory is looked at every 2 seconds instead of several times a second.
- **Checked again, everything else rests.** An idle chat, a very long chat after you scroll it, the sync panel, Hearth
  behind another app and Hearth minimized ask for no screen frames at all, and nothing keeps moving after a reply ends.

## 🚀 Ready to use sooner
- Hearth is ready a little sooner after you open it: your chats list and the chat you were in come up about a tenth of
  a second earlier on a big profile.
- Checking your Claude and Codex installs, and setting your show and leave reminders, now happens a moment after the
  window is ready instead of while it's opening. Reminders still ring on time.
- Nothing looks or works differently.
