# Round 138: a mood for each section, and your favourite controls
*November 2026*

## 🎭 Give each section its mood
- In the timeline's **Sections ▾**, under ✦ Map: **🎭 Give each section its mood**. Each part of the song gets a
  mood: from your brief's plan when it names the part ("verse calm, drop punchy"), else calm intros and breaks,
  rising builds, punchy drops, fading outros. It turns your mood dials at each part's first beat and puts a fitting
  saved look on its cue (no dials yet? it offers to suggest some first, free).
- It shows dashed on the timeline and in a list first: **Keep all**, **Keep this section**, or ✕ one item; each keep
  is one Undo. Change a part's mood from its small menu. **✦ Ask the AI to refine** shows its cost first.

## ↶ Undo right after an AI reply
- Ctrl/⌘+Z pressed the moment an AI reply finishes now takes back that reply, never the step before it. If the
  scene is still settling it waits a moment ("Settling…") instead of guessing.

## ★ Favourite controls
- Right-click any slider (or a dial's ⋯) › **★ Pin to favourites**. Your pinned controls, from any layer, sit in one
  small panel behind the **★** in the Sliders panel's head (folded until you open it; also Ctrl/⌘+K). Each keeps its
  layer's name, range, lock, music link and ◆ key; drag ⋮⋮ to reorder, ✕ to unpin. Every move is one Undo.
- **✦ Suggest** picks the sliders you change most (free). In **Perform**, a ★ on the strip opens them; on the phone
  remote they show as a "★ Favourites" group. The AI sees which ones you pinned.
