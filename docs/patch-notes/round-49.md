# Round 49: a thriftier director, smooth scrubbing and scrolling, a calmer app, native keys on Mac and PC
*October 2026*

## 🎬 The Three Director gets it done in fewer steps
- On six typical asks (a beat layer, a fix, a palette, a camera move, a 9:16 version, a tweak + still) it went from 32 steps to 18, and about half the tokens.
- Changes that show on screen come back with a small picture, so it no longer takes a separate screenshot step. No picture when nothing visibly changed or something broke.
- Errors come with the usual fix and the lines around them, so fixes are often one step. A colour change across the scene is one edit.
- It doesn't re-read the same how-to twice in a chat.
- Turn the automatic pictures off in ⋯ AI setup › Habits.

## ⏯ Scrubbing the Lab timeline is instant
- The preview follows your pointer as fast as it can draw and never falls behind; when you let go it shows exactly that frame.
- Zooming and scrolling the waveform are much lighter.
- New: you hear the song under the playhead while you drag it. A click to jump stays silent; turn it off in ▶'s right-click menu.
- Fix: Shift+wheel scrolls the timeline sideways on Windows.

## 🌀 Smoother scrolling
- Settings, the Usage window and the patch notes scroll much more smoothly: they no longer re-blur the screen behind them every frame. Dialogs look a touch more solid.
- Tooltips no longer pop up while you scroll with the pointer resting.
- The mood board pans and zooms with less work; a chat scrolls lighter while a reply streams.

## 🧘 A calmer app
- The rail lost two buttons: ⛶ Full screen and the Board drawer are in its ⋯ (they stay in the rail while on).
- One name everywhere: **Find anything** (Ctrl/⌘+K) and **Usage**.
- Notes come at most three at a time; × shows when you point at one. Errors keep their fix and ▸ Details in sight.
- The Usage window shows five tabs, with More ▾ for the rest.
- Nothing was removed: Ctrl/⌘+K finds everything that moved.

## 🍎🪟 Keys that feel native
- Shortcuts are written your computer's way everywhere: ⇧⌘K on the Mac, Ctrl+Shift+K on Windows.
- On the Mac, keys macOS keeps for itself are shown with the Control key that works: ⌃Tab, ⌃⌥D (Duo), ⌃Space (voice).
- AZERTY / QWERTZ on Windows: AltGr types @ # { [ | \ € µ again and never runs a shortcut by accident.
- Letter shortcuts follow the letter printed on your key. German/French Mac: ⌥ types @ [ ] { } | in text fields.
- The board's mouse wheel zooms again at Windows 150 %; Projects › "A file…" picks files on Windows.

## Try it
- **Mac:** press ⌃Tab — the agent before comes back. Open the keys sheet (⌘/) — no "Ctrl" anywhere.
- **PC with AZERTY/QWERTZ:** type AltGr+à (or AltGr+Q) in a chat — @ appears and nothing else happens.
- **Lab:** drag the playhead fast across a song — you hear it, and the picture keeps up.
