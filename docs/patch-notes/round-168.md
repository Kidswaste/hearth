# Round 168: Forgeheart draws lighter
*November 2026*

## Forgeheart chats draw less

- **The Forgeheart look's soft lights over a chat are now drawn only where they actually shine** (the warm glow at the
  top and the violet ember in the bottom-right corner), not over the whole chat. Each frame in a Forgeheart chat costs
  the graphics side about 25 % less, about 10 % less with Settings open or while scrolling. It looks exactly the same.

## A check fixed

- A built-in check said the board item's menu had lost its order; the menu was fine, the check read a shortened copy.
