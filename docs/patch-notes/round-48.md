# Round 48: usage left, a calmer and faster Lab, a lighter Hearth, fixes
*October 2026*

## 📊 Usage left
- The Usage window (click the token meter) now starts with one card per AI: **Claude's** 5-hour session and week, **Astra's** session and week — how much is left and when each resets.
- A heads-up says how long you have at your pace: "At your pace this week you'll reach the weekly limit around Thu 6 pm". When most of a limit is used it says so plainly: "84% used; 16% left until Oct 31".
- **How to save from now on**: the 3 changes that save the most for you, each one click with Undo.
- A calm note at 80 % and 95 % of a limit, and when it resets. Nothing is ever blocked.
- 0 tokens: the numbers come from what Claude Code and Codex already report. Every number says how fresh it is; if Hearth doesn't know yet, it says so. Also `/usage-left`.

## 🧘 A calmer Lab
- At rest the picture's pill shows Freeze and your frame sizes; More…, Safe zones, Still and ⋯ appear when you point at the picture.
- A 9:16 or 16:9 frame no longer sits under that pill — the top of your picture stays in view.
- With the director docked, the toolbar stays on one row.
- A layer's timing (start, end, fades, Animate) is one folded row, "When it plays".
- "lighter to stay smooth" shows for a few seconds, then shrinks to a small ◐.
- Nothing was removed: Lab search and Ctrl/⌘+K find everything that moved.

## 🚀 A faster Lab
- Switching to a scene with pictures, models or videos happens in one go — no half-built picture first.
- Opening the Lab gets your scene running sooner (about 1.9 s → 0.9 s in our test).
- With a song, the preview no longer jumps in size a moment after opening.
- 3D models load quicker the first time.

## 🪶 Lighter over a long day
- An hour of switching chats and scenes now grows by about 1 MB, down from about 24 MB.
- The Lab no longer keeps old scenes alive behind the scenes after a switch; deleted chats leave nothing behind.

## 🔧 Fixes
- Particle sparks that burst on a kick stay on screen after a slow frame.
- `/astra-tools on` / `off` gives an Astra director its hub tools again; `/astra-tools` alone opens the Astra tools window.
- `/this-computer` is ready right away after start.

## Good to know
- History shows a scene's older versions about a second after the Lab opens.
- The usage forecast is an estimate from your recent pace.
