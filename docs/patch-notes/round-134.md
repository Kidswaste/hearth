# Round 134: effect blocks, follow the music, and my work
*November 2026*

## 🧩 Effect blocks
- ⋯ › **🧩 Blocks** (or ✦ More, or `/blocks`) opens a small card of 12 blocks, each with a live preview: noise field,
  glow, trails, kaleidoscope, particles, displacement, colour split, film grain, pixelate, mirror, colour ramp and
  beat strobe. Click one to add it above the selected layer, in your palette (one Undo).
- The chain lists your blocks: drag to reorder (one Undo). **💾 Save as my block** keeps a chain under "Mine".
- **✦ Combine for me**: type a mood ("dreamy underwater pulse") and the AI picks and sets 3–5 blocks (cost shown first,
  one Undo).

## 🎚 Follow the music
- Right-click a slider › **🎚 Follow the music…** (or the Sliders panel's ⋯). Pick Bass, Low-mids, Mids, Highs, Kick,
  Snare, Loudness, or drag a custom range on the small live spectrum; a live meter shows it and the slider moves as a
  preview. Set the range, Smooth / Punchy / On the beat, Invert, then **Keep** (one Undo).
- It guesses a sensible band from the slider's name (glow → bass, speed → loudness, sparkle → highs). A small 〰 mark
  shows linked sliders; click it to change. Renders come out exactly like the preview.

## ★ My work
- ＋ New project › Start from › **★ My work…**, the Lab's ＋ Add › **★ From my work…**, or ★ My work in the sketch
  browser: your past projects' scenes, templates, standout layers (ones you rendered or kept a long time), palettes and
  saved looks, with pictures, search and filters.
- **Start a new scene from this** (your original is never changed), **Add this layer here** (recoloured to this
  project's palette, one Undo), **Use this look** (one Undo).
- **✦ Like my past work**: the AI reads a short summary of your library (titles, palettes, kinds of layers; no code or
  pictures) and suggests 3 starting points for this project (cost shown first).
