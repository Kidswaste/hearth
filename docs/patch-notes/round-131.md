# Round 131: a layer like this, render presets, and Perform
*November 2026*

## ✦ A layer like this picture
- On a picture in the Lab's 🖼 References (**✦ Like this**), on a moodboard picture (right-click › **✦ Make a layer
  like this**), or when you drop a picture on the preview, the director builds **one new layer in its spirit**: its
  shapes, motion, light and colours, with sliders for the key settings. It doesn't paste the picture in (unless you
  tick that).
- A small card shows the picture, its colours and the cost first; nothing is sent until you click **✦ Make the
  layer**. Afterwards: **↶ Undo** (one step), **Closer** or **Looser** for another pass, or **Keep**. Tick "Use the
  project's palette" to keep your project's colours. Also `/like <picture>`.

## ⇪ Render presets
- At the top of the Render card, one-click presets: **Vertical reel** (9:16, built around the song's drop),
  **Seamless loop**, **Full song**, **4K master** (upright on a vertical project) and **Square post**. Each fills
  the card; you can still change anything. Tick several to render them one after another.
- **＋ Save as preset** keeps your own settings with the project (★ chips). The project menu's "Render the main
  scene…" opens on the last preset you used. Files are named "project – preset – date"; a name that exists asks
  before replacing.

## ◉ Perform
- Right-click **▣ Present** › **◉ Perform** (or `/perform`): the scene fills your second screen at full speed, with
  a small strip on yours.
- **1–9** jump to the song's parts (the cues from Map to the song), landing on the next bar or beat (press 0 to
  choose). **Q, W, E…** switch saved looks. **Space** freezes, **B** fades to black, **T** taps a tempo, **Esc**
  leaves. 🎛 learns a MIDI controller's pads.
- Nothing is saved unless you tick **● Rec**: then your switches go on the timeline as cues, and one Undo takes them
  back. The director can prepare a set for you to review ("prepare a set for my show").
