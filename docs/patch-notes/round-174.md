# Round 174: a fresh look at what Hearth costs
*November 2026*

## Settings and Hearth's own tests

- **Opening Settings no longer re-times your screen every time**: the graphics line measures it once and remembers it
  until the window moves to another screen. Everything looks the same.
- Hearth's built-in tests no longer sit idle for 30 seconds at each step, so the "pick up where you left off" test
  passes again (the card itself was fine).
