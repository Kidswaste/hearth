# Round 76: projects across both computers, play performance, smoother Lab, clicks that land
*October 2026*

## 📁 Projects across your two computers
- Projects now travel properly between your PC and your Mac. Open a project's Desk: under its name a small line says
  "All here · synced 2 min ago", or "3 files still arriving from Mac" while things are on their way. Click it for the
  sync panel.
- Open a project you just made on your other computer before everything has arrived and Hearth now says "Still
  arriving from your Mac…" and opens it by itself as soon as it lands, instead of showing an error.
- If you delete a project on one computer while you were still working in it on the other, nothing is lost: it stays,
  and its Desk asks whether to keep it or delete it. If you had only looked at it, the delete simply goes through.
- If the project you have open gets deleted on your other computer, a note tells you, with a Restore button.
- Things you put on a project's worktable shelf, or storyboard shots you edit, on both computers now all stay.
- Opening the same project on both computers no longer causes a sync conflict.
- Where you left off in a project (tab, chat, scene) and "Keep the Lab ready" stay separate on each computer.
- Install this update on both computers.

## 📈 Play performance on your graphics card
- **📈 Test play performance**: right-click ▶ Play (or press F in a game and click **📈 Test performance**). Hearth
  plays your game for 20 seconds and tells you in plain words how fast it runs compared with your screen, whether your
  graphics card or your processor is the limit, which graphics card and drawing path it really uses, and what Hearth
  itself costs.
- **One-click fixes** right on the card when one applies: switch the graphics path to D3D11, use the fast graphics
  card, turn Quality: auto on, try Graphics: Balanced, or open Windows' display settings with the exact tip (main
  display, 240 Hz).
- **📋 Copy report for Claude** puts every number on your clipboard to paste into a chat; nothing is sent anywhere.
  The last report stays under right-click ▶ Play › **📈 Last performance report**.
- **HTML games now use your fast graphics card** in ▶ Play (they could land on the built-in one before).

## 🎮 Smoother Lab
- Opening the Lab on a scene without music no longer borrows the song you last played in another scene. Before, the
  song quietly attached itself to that scene, and the timeline popped open a moment after the Lab appeared.
- When a scene's song takes a moment to load, the timeline now keeps its full height from the start. The preview no
  longer jumps smaller after it appears.
- The Lab preview and ▶ Play make much less memory garbage per frame again. That means fewer tiny pauses for memory
  clean-up, which you can notice as hitches at 240 Hz. ▶ Play is down by about a third, and the Lab preview by about a
  fifth.

## 🖱 Clicks and jumps that land
- **Find in chat and "jump to message" now land on the message and stay there.** In a long chat, finding a word in
  an old message no longer throws you back to the end a moment later, and jumping to an early message shows it at
  once (about four times faster) and keeps it still while pictures around it load.
- **The ⋯ button closes AI setup again.** The AI setup panel no longer covers the ⋯ that opened it, so clicking ⋯ a
  second time closes it, and the ◎ button underneath works again.
- **"✎ Note (F8)" on the game's stats chip works with the mouse.** The chip's buttons no longer shift when
  "Quality" appears, so clicking ✎ Note makes a note (it used to switch Quality instead). Clicks on Hearth's own
  buttons over a game are never passed to the game.
